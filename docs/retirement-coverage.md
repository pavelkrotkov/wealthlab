# Retirement coverage and limits

Reviewed October 25, 2026 against the five acceptance criteria in [issue #6](https://github.com/pavelkrotkov/wealthlab/issues/6). Coverage means executable inputs, calculations, UI and checks within the declared profiles below. It does not mean every possible combination of tax history, wrapper and benefit event is implemented. The feature ticket can close after the current implementation passes CI/review and merges; broader optimization remains in issue #18.

See [contributions](adr/0016-future-contribution-assumptions.md), [IRA household integration](adr/0015-household-ira.md), [plan RMDs](adr/0048-workplace-rmd.md) and [designated Roth cash](adr/0053-designated-roth-plan-cash.md). |

See [survivors](adr/0011-survivor-benefits.md), [work tests](adr/0056-household-social-security-work.md), [Medicare](adr/0017-household-medicare.md), [medical bills](adr/0039-current-medical-bills.md), [nonqualified HSA](adr/0037-household-hsa-distribution-tax.md) and [education](adr/0023-household-education.md). |

## Explicit boundaries

The survivor workbench is implemented separately from household death transitions. Changes to household ownership, filing status and accounts after a death are not automatically modeled. The household work-test profile supports own-record benefits, complete earned-W2 histories and claims beginning within the projection. It excludes auxiliary/survivor work effects, opening work-related positions, self-employment, special payments and simultaneous Medicare SSA withholding. The separate existing no-work Medicare-withholding profile remains available.

Inherited/special wrapper interests, uncovered exceptions and rollover histories require their own adapters. The HSA profile uses the monthly eligibility method; it does not infer last-month-rule eligibility. Nonqualified and other-purpose 529 household distributions remain outside the qualified-tuition adapter. Standalone workbenches do not silently authorize household use of an unsupported combination. Each relevant ADR records finer limits and source dates.

OWL comparisons are limited to the adapter's validated cases. Broader income, returns, couples, partial state basis and other wrapper comparisons are follow-on reference coverage, not a requirement to reproduce every native scenario in OWL. Earlier ADR language describing these expansions as remaining work under #6 is superseded by this acceptance map. The implemented controls must continue to reject unsupported profiles.

Rule assumptions and conditional NJ treatment remain visible in inputs/results. This coverage audit does not validate personal eligibility, replace the source-specific rule checks, or establish retirement safety through age100 from the short synthetic examples.
