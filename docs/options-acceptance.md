# Options acceptance and supported scope

Issue [#10](https://github.com/pavelkrotkov/wealthlab/issues/10) is implemented in three dependent slices: [dated contracts](adr/0070-dated-options.md), [actual household funding](adr/0071-household-options.md) and [bounded realized-market execution](adr/0072-options-market-paths.md). All examples are original synthetic inputs. Conditional assumptions are inputs, not a conclusion about a particular contract's legal treatment.

The market adapter is intentionally monthly: contracts open at the horizon start; observed expiries, exercise and dividend dates must be supported month ends. It rejects future entries/rolls without new execution quotes, intramonth dates, selected-factor sale locks, nonzero generated cash yields, zero-rounded prices and stale bindings. It never predicts American assignment. Fixed implied volatility/rates/continuous pricing yields are supplied assumptions, not a volatility surface or discrete-dividend exercise model. Derivative-adjusted issuer concentration remains explicitly unmeasured.

The standalone book retains unresolved delivery and tax status visibly. A household projection cannot continue beyond a failed expired delivery until an explicit resolution adapter exists. It also rejects unsupported tax histories and shared ownership of an option account with another product book. Explicit negative equity remains a household liability even after all stock is sold; an unpaid cash settlement is not silently reclassified as a fee or as an elected loan.

These boundaries satisfy the issue's explicit conditional-treatment and unsupported-period requirements. They do not establish general legal eligibility, certified broker margin, historical option performance, daily liquidity or a guarantee of spendable household wealth. Required repository CI and exact-head review remain integration gates.
