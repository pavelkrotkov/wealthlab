# ADR 0120: Explicit annual Puerto Rico source and residence comparison

Status: accepted implementation boundary, 2026-10-25. Issue #15; monthly integration remains #17.

## Decision

Reuse the existing annual US/NJ baseline and public Tax-Calculator adapter. Add a separate PR annual case and Annual tax subworkbench. No new dependency or territory framework is needed. Original synthetic receipts reconcile exactly to the baseline federal income. Federal recognition, PR recognition, federal source and PR source are separate inputs; a US qualified dividend is not automatically a PR preferential dividend.

The initial supported profile is positive ordinary income, single/joint adults below 65 without dependents, with matched supplied US/PR filing status (joint assumes spouses living together), with allocated federal standard deduction and independently supplied allowed regular/ABT PR deductions. PR regular brackets, the statutory gradual adjustment cap, the 92%/95% reduction and flat ABT bands are calculated separately. Wage-only ABT exemption requires an explicit wage-informative reporting claim. This is the ordinary-rate election, before unsupported special/refundable PR credits, not a completed return or automatic minimum over preferential elections. Prior ABT credit/carry consumption, optional separate-spouse computation, direct expenses, business/pass-through income, losses, retirement and senior/child credits are not implemented in this profile.

Federal bona fide residence uses the bounded 183-day route. Tax home, closer connection, citizenship and move-year history are supplied reviewed/assumed claims. Other presence tests are not silently approximated. Move-in requires explicit prior-three and prospective-next-three history; departure uses the US-citizen/prior-two-year exception. Assumed facts keep the result conditional. Missing/failed conditions withhold the PR candidate while the mainland baseline remains runnable. Local worldwide-income dates remain independent of federal BFR status. Receipts outside local residence are included only when PR-source.

Section 933-excluded income reduces the standard deduction using taxable/worldwide gross income. Fresh public Policy/Records/Calculator objects prevent case-specific deduction changes from mutating shared cached policy. Raw original wages supply employee payroll exactly once, including additional Medicare. US-government wages retain their federal exception.

The bounded current-year US foreign credit supports ordinary/general-category income with explicitly allocated paid/accrued PR income tax. It cannot offset NIIT or payroll. Preferential/AMT/separate passive-basket paths guard rather than substitute an ordinary ratio; evidence is required and claimed current-year PR tax cannot exceed calculated liability. PR Schedule C is calculated after US credit, with separate regular and ABT limitations and a reduction for federal income excluded locally. No prior foreign-credit carries or treaty re-sourcing are inferred. PR credit eligibility for the US NIIT is a distinct unresolved classification; affected positive-credit profiles are withheld instead of assuming the NIIT is eligible or ineligible.

An annual federal/PR/payroll comparison does not establish a household relocation benefit. NJ baseline stays visible and independent; no zero-state-tax relocation savings are assumed. Actual state departure liability, monthly household funding and optimizer linkage remain #17. All inputs and audit output retain their dates, supplied classifications, claim states and explicit limitations.

## Dated authorities and independent checks

- [26 USC 933](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title26-section933&num=0&edition=prelim): full-year PR-source exclusion, government-service exception and citizen departure rule; verified 2026-10-25.
- [Hacienda 2025 individual instructions, official index](https://hacienda.pr.gov/node/5770), [English guide](https://hacienda.pr.gov/sites/default/files/inst_individuals_2025.pdf), [Spanish guide revised February 27, 2026](https://hacienda.pr.gov/sites/default/files/inst_individuos_2025_27_feb_262.pdf): local move-year reporting, personal exemption, Schedule C and separate ABT credit computation. PDF metadata can show an older year; the document's printed 2025 year governs.
- [PR Internal Revenue Code, consolidated revision May 28, 2026](https://bvirtualogp.pr.gov/ogp/Bvirtual/leyesreferencia/PDF/C%C3%B3digos/1-2011/1-2011.pdf), §§1021.01, 1021.02 and 1051.01: regular brackets, $8,895 + 33% of exemptions gradual-adjustment cap, reduction and ABT/foreign-credit rules.

The local QMD tax-references collection was searched first. Its related federal materials did not establish current Act 60 or PR computation; the official documents above supply the decisive passages. No private taxpayer records were searched or indexed.

Independent numeric tests cover regular bracket boundaries, $100,000 reduction discontinuity, the explicit gradual cap, whole-base ABT thresholds, separate credit limitations, original-wage payroll, source reconciliation, government wages, move-in/departure dates and the Pub 570 deduction allocation. Real AppTest exercises submission and malformed configuration recovery in both annual workbenches.

The core does not infer a decree or implement Act 60 benefits. Dated incentive/gain computation belongs to its dependent owning layer.
