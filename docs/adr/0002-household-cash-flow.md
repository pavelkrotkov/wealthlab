# ADR 0002: Household cash-flow foundation

Status: implemented first slice. Optional tax integration is now described in [ADR 0003](0003-tax-cash-flow.md); the original no-tax behavior below remains available when the tax plan is disabled.

The scenario has an explicit schema version, one or two members, inclusive start/exclusive end, accounts, monthly income and expense schedules, debts, contributions, currency rates, input provenance and year-to-date context. Required unknown amounts are not replaced with zero. Unresolved records block projection. The public starter is deliberately synthetic.

Money is validated to cents and computed with Decimal, rounding monthly accruals to cents using half-even. Reports use USD; entered currency amounts are converted with explicit fixed USD-per-unit rates. FX paths belong to the later cross-border/path modules. All dates and units are preserved in JSON. Named income ends at its owner's retirement date when configured; partial-month accrual uses actual overlapping days divided by that calendar month's days. Income and expenses grow from the projection start at their individual annual growth assumptions. Reporting inflation independently converts ending balances to start-date purchasing power.

Within a month, income becomes available before payments. Cash accounts fund debt service, account fees, essential spending, healthcare, mortgage budget, education, discretionary spending and then internal contributions, in that order. Account order controls cash depletion. This monthly convention does not establish intramonth liquidity. Non-cash assets remain at opening value plus contributions, without assumed liquidation or return. Household taxes are not yet computed: gross wages and the absence of new tax payments are visibly identified beside every projection. Do not interpret this slice as an after-tax retirement plan.

Debt interest uses APR / 12 and the partial-month fraction. Payment reduces interest first, then principal; unpaid interest remains in the debt balance. Unpaid fees remain a separate obligation deducted from net wealth and are paid ahead of new fees when cash becomes available. Unmet consumption is reported as a spending shortfall, not a negative cash balance or invented loan. Contributions use only remaining cash and increase the destination account; they are never counted as an expense. Specify mortgage payments in either debt service or a budget as appropriate; entering the same obligation in both double-counts it. Contract-specific arrears, penalties and maturity/default provisions remain outside this simple debt model.

The reserve target annualizes the current period's required spending, debt payments and fees, multiplied by the configured reserve years. It excludes contributions and unimplemented taxes. The 95% success and younger-member age 100 targets are stored, but neither simulation success nor an automatic lifespan horizon is claimed; the explicit end date controls this slice.

Year-to-date income and paid federal/state taxes describe activity already reflected in opening balances; they are retained for the future annual tax reconciler and never reapplied as new cash flows. A scenario saved and reloaded must produce identical rows and input hash. Keep a baseline, edit the scenario and project again for a comparison; mismatched date ranges block the comparison.

JSON uploads stay in memory. CSV table imports replace that table on submission; remove the selected CSV before editing its imported rows. Saves require an explicitly entered existing private directory outside the public repository. A fully written, fsynced temporary file is atomically linked to a new filename; existing files and symlinks cannot be overwritten. Temporary save files live in that same selected directory. The app does not create a private directory on the user's behalf or load adviser records automatically.

Checks cover independent no-return cash conservation, retirement within a partial month, nominal/real outputs, currency conversion, debt/transfer accounting, funding failure, validation failures, atomic no-overwrite round-trips and actual Streamlit form submission. Later tax, retirement, product and path slices must preserve these accounting identities.
