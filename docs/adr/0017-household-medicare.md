# ADR 0017: Direct-billed Medicare in household cash and completed-return history

Status: another layer of open retirement ticket #6. Connects the Medicare premium engine to household cash, healthcare spending, liabilities and completed federal tax results. [Current-premium benefit collection and SMI rounding](0018-household-benefit-premiums.md) now have a separately confirmed adapter.

The household plan embeds confirmed Medicare coverage and an explicit frozen/indexed future premium scenario. Its members and complete calendar-year horizon must match the household. Separate confirmations establish direct billing, complete household tax history and exclusion of these same premiums from other expense entries. Standalone opening cash/receipts cannot enter this shared wallet. The direct-bill profile rejects Social Security cash; generated claims can instead use the separately confirmed current-premium collection profile. Separate-return residency status and conditional current-income redeterminations remain in the standalone workbench.

Only pre-projection income history may be supplied. The initial required two-year lookbacks are checked before projection; an explicitly confirmed three-year fallback remains available when its normal return is absent. Subsequent returns are generated from completed household federal AGI plus tax-exempt interest, including IRA year-end basis reconciliation, conversions and other supported household income. A static low-income forecast cannot replace those generated years. Joint MAGI is shared rather than divided between enrolled spouses. Generated history is runtime-only; exported original inputs retain their prior records and reproduce the same result.

Premium quotes reuse the existing Medicare enrollment, table, part-selection and conditional growth calculations. Quoted bills join healthcare funding after essential spending and before other healthcare entries. Taxable-account funding requests include both new bills and standing premium arrears, subject to the existing explicitly permitted funding sources. No unscheduled IRA/HSA withdrawal or borrowing is inferred. Actual premium payments are included once in healthcare spending; unpaid bills remain liabilities that reduce net wealth. Current missed amounts enter funding failures once, while later cash pays oldest bills first. The audit retains each original due shortfall and its later payment month. Coverage persists as supplied during a cash stress; cancellation or reinstatement is not predicted.

This is a monthly household budget. First-of-month bill/payment labels do not certify intramonth cash sufficiency or actual Medicare billing dates. Paying an old bill does not inflate the recurring reserve target: reserve demand removes payments curing prior arrears. HSA reimbursement and medical deductions are not generated. Future Medicare costs remain explicit assumptions, and generated future scenario returns are not IRS filing records or SSA determinations. [ADR 0009](0009-medicare.md) retains the underlying premium scope.

## Reproducible synthetic cases

The UI example begins with $250,000 cash and $500,000 in an ordinary IRA. Fixed withdrawals total $60,000 over three years, while a $250,000 conversion stays in Roth. Prior joint MAGI is $301,000 for 2024 and $121,000 for 2025. One person is enrolled for all 2026 and the second from July, giving 18 enrolled-person months in that year, followed by 24 each subsequent year. Supplied drug-plan and other-health costs are $40 and $200 monthly.

Frozen-2026 premiums total $12,299.40 in 2026 and $10,629.60 in 2027. The completed 2026 household AGI of $270,000 produces $12,926.40 premiums in 2028 even though that year's AGI is only $20,000. Removing the conversion instead produces $10,629.60 in 2028, a $2,296.80 difference attributable to the two-year lookback. Adding $5,000 exempt interest leaves federal AGI unchanged but raises 2026 MAGI to $275,000, crossing the next premium boundary.

The complete baseline reconciles $750,000 opening wealth less $144,000 ordinary spending, $55,067.70 modeled federal/NJ taxes and $35,855.40 premiums to $515,076.90. Ending cash is $75,076.90, traditional IRA $190,000 and Roth $250,000. The conversion is neither another receipt nor a capital loss. Taxes use the household's explicitly frozen-2025 assumptions, independently of the premium rule base.

A separate cash stress has no initial cash, no other spending and only a supplied $1,000 March receipt. March pays three $202.90 bills, including $405.80 of arrears. The current two-year reserve remains $4,869.60 rather than tripling. Original cash gaps total $3,058, while later payments reduce the final unpaid premium liability to $2,652.20; the initial failures remain visible. Standing IRA assets cannot hide that cash failure.

Checks cover these paired choices, exempt-interest MAGI, original due failures, FIFO payments, arrears liabilities, reserve demand, account restrictions, missing/future/duplicate history, confirmations, original-input immutability, JSON reproduction, one-person starters and the runnable UI.
