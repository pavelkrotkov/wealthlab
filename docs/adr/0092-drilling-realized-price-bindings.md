# Realized commodity and liquid-reference prices for drilling

The optional `HouseholdBinding.drilling` connects one actual drilling account to
two independently configured existing market factors. One factor supplies the
commodity selling-price trajectory; the other supplies the actual liquid-share
reference price and dealing locks. The adapter uses the existing realized batch,
household compiler and native drilling/reference books. It is not a new return,
well-valuation, legal-eligibility or tax calculation engine.

## Explicit fixed-fact envelope

The binding supplies an opening commodity price and confirms replacement of the
entered price observations. The liquid reference retains its separately supplied
opening share price. A required independent source description and confirmation
cover the validity of **all fixed production quantities, costs, calls, tax facts,
residual-property values and exit quotes** across these realized price paths.
Those fields remain unchanged. A cheaper commodity does not automatically
revalue the well, adjust its sale quote, recalculate depletion or revise supplied
SE/QBI/AMT or other legal limitations. Unknown/unconfirmed envelope validity blocks
the run, rather than silently extending a fixed illustration to every price path.

This confirmation is a conditional input, not an appraisal or verified legal
finding. [ADR0091](0091-conditional-drilling-partnership.md) and its separately
supplied federal/NJ recognition, limitations and history remain authoritative.
Changing employment, income, funded costs or other candidate decisions requires
an independently supported candidate envelope too; that optimizer validation is
a separate owning concern. `full_tax_optimization_supported` remains false.

## Observations, cash and restrictions

Each month compounds the supplied opening price by that factor's observed price
return. Commodity quotes round to cents; share-reference marks retain eight
decimals. No future return changes an earlier quote. Actual production times
that commodity price enters the existing cash-basis book, with unchanged costs,
creditors, distributions, recourse and independently supplied tax facts. Actual
reference purchases/sales retain their live shares, holding dates and separate
federal/NJ bases. No capital or tax-saving cash is generated by the binding.

The two factors must be distinct known columns with the existing separate
price-plus-cash convention and zero factor cash yields. The adapter does not
invent dividends for the price-only reference or treat a financial factor's
yield as physical production. Locked commodity-sales observations require a
separate receipt/settlement adapter and are rejected. Commodity price may reach
zero, yielding no production receipt. Nonpositive share prices require a share
write-off adapter and are rejected. Reference market locks are added to existing
entered restrictions; they cannot unlock a contract or provide sale cash from a
locked marked value. Existing whole-month, exact-horizon and ownership checks
remain in effect.

Residual well equity is not asserted to have unit exposure to the commodity
factor. Neither this price binding nor a positive marked account supplies a
complete issuer-concentration inventory. Unmeasured exposure remains disclosed.

## Matched comparison and local UI

Products / **Drilling partnerships** retains its entered-price workflow and adds
deterministic, invented historical, Monte Carlo and stress price methods. The
editable JSON contains the actual household, complete existing market model and
explicit binding/source confirmation. One generated batch is reused for drilling
and the separately configured liquid reference on each path. Native outcome
reporting retains actual household cash/taxes, product funding failures and joint
funding feasibility; it does not replace those with a factor-growth result.
Complete inputs, batch and actual cost/basis/creditor/tax/exit audits export together.
Unsupported edits clear the prior result. Synthetic factors, history and fixed
well quotes are original assumptions, not sponsor forecasts or observed history.

## Independent checks

Two observed monthly shocks give commodity quotes $110 then $99 from $100, while
the independent reference stays at $100 then rises to $120. A ten-unit second-month
production receipt is exactly $990. The supplied $200 operating cost and $40,000
full property sale leave actual $40,790 exit cash and $140,790 household cash after
the original $100,000 call. The reference buys actual shares and receives $120,000
on sale; its original $200,000 capital plus $20,000 appreciation is conserved
before actual tax. Production/cost/tax/property/exit records are otherwise unchanged.

A reference exit lock retains $120,000 marked shares but supplies zero sale cash
and fails native joint funding. Existing locks persist on an otherwise liquid
path. Zero commodity price produces no revenue while the independently supplied
property exit quote stays unchanged. Future shocks cannot rewrite an earlier
observation. All four market methods, editable UI submission, stale-result clearing
and missing/unknown/unsupported binding combinations have runnable checks.
