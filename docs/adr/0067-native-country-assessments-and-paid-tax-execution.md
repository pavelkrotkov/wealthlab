# 0067 — Native country assessments and actual paid-tax execution

Status: accepted implementation boundary; conditional profiles, not universal cross-border coverage.

## Decision

Reuse the native PR and Spain annual engines, the existing household projection and its single foreign-tax book. The editable Cross-border / Country cash workbench binds computed country liabilities to stable assessments in that book. It executes actual cash, source withholding and fees once. It does not create another payment engine or replace the independent NJ profile.

Prepared PR federal execution retains worldwide dated source facts for payroll and NJ. The existing section 933 eligibility/source policy excludes only observed eligible receipts. The same prepared Tax-Calculator policy prorates the standard deduction, then applies native `e07300`/`e62900` credit inputs once; its legacy custom credit subtraction is bypassed. Excluded income cannot create US source-limit capacity, foreign credit or an excess carry. The native PR local calculation receives remaining US income tax actually paid after the federal credit, excluding employee payroll and NIIT. Unknown income/payroll payment allocations reject.

A dedicated prepared-PR calculator uses the existing lock and public Tax-Calculator APIs. Its identity includes the installed policy year and fixed ordinary-income reform. Every call restores the original standard-deduction vector before applying its exact current source ratio and refreshes every input/calculated record array. It does not modify the ordinary federal engine or its result cache. Fresh-policy reference, alternating-ratio, changing-credit and thread-isolation checks cover reuse.

A read-only projection observer receives detached copies of authoritative closed returns after monthly settlement and after terminal wash/product reconciliation. Execution order is preserved; separate audit histories are not sorted to guess the latest return. The PR closing assessment reads its actual December31 snapshot. Later-year US arrears/refunds or return changes require a dated PR reassessment and reject here, rather than backdating additional paid-tax credit. The country consumer performs bounded fresh-scenario reconciliation passes for reciprocal paid-tax effects. Only the converged execution is returned. Nonconvergence raises an explicit error, clearing earlier UI results and exports.

Spain retains person/year/source and separate IRPF, IP and solidarity-tax assessment identities. Its pure native assessment consumes actual observed payment records from the shared foreign book. Scheduled IP creates no solidarity credit. The workbench recalculates that credit only after actual recognized IP payment. Every ordinary source has an explicit US person/year/source link; unknown sources, mismatched recognition dates and unsupported treaty source differences reject. Spanish foreign-credit relief is explicitly denied in the current cash comparison; an unresolved claim cannot silently become zero or an eligibility benefit.

Actual bank cash, levy currency and country-recognized applied tax remain separate. A USD bank payment can settle an EUR levy while its recognized country amount excludes penalties. Each amount retains its actual date and sourced spot quote. Refund cash uses receipt-date FX; US credit reversal retains the original payment-date FX. A confirmed country-recognition reversal cannot exceed its original applied amount. No annual FX scalar or US lot basis is silently substituted.

## Evidence and boundaries

The prepared PR exclusion/deduction policy and ordinary PR credit allocation reuse [ADR0120](0120-puerto-rico-annual-source-and-residence.md); dated incentive/basis rules remain in [ADR0121](0121-puerto-rico-dated-decree-and-stock-gains.md). Spain assessments and observed-payment semantics reuse [ADR0140](0140-conditional-spanish-annual-assessments.md), [ADR0141](0141-common-regime-spanish-regional-policy.md) and [ADR0142](0142-spanish-assessment-obligation-bridge.md). The US paid-method/refund translation follows final [2025 Form1116 instructions, page3](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i1116--2025.pdf) and [2025 Publication514, page7](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/p514--2025.pdf). Previously paid-year liability and subsequent original-tax refunds remain distinct.

Checks execute actual monthly earned wages without recognizing future income, native federal/PR credit order, zero/partial actual US tax, actual country payments, paid-IP solidarity relief, gross-bank-cash versus applied-country-tax and stale-result clearing in both country UI modes. A future stock sale is not recognized early; a realized gain lacking the native dated preferential/source profile rejects the candidate.

The current workbench supports ordinary PR and supplied full-year common-regime Spain assessment facts. It does not claim complete issue17 coverage. Preferential/decree/gain source and basis execution, treaty resourcing, independent AMT allocation, complete later-year original-return redeterminations/carryback, independent NJ prepayment/refund positions and unknown foreign-product/funding-gain classifications remain guarded or subsequent integration work. A full-year closing assessment cannot be omitted by shortening the projection. Legal residence, source classifications, frozen future law, sponsor terms, actual-date FX and prelimited deductions remain explicit assumptions.
