# ADR 0004: Tax lots and harvesting

Status: #5 lot workbench merged in PR #26 after current-head CI and J-Bot review passed. Household cash integration remains a required dependency for the matched path and release slices.

Lots carry quantity, acquisition date, federal holding start, liquidity date and separate total federal/NJ basis. Quantities are units, basis is a total dollar amount, and trade prices are per unit. Every account has explicit ownership and wrapper. The current workbench is long-only and USD-only; it does not interpret a retirement-account lot basis as a deductible IRA contribution or tax basis in distributions.

Opening positions must exist by the opening date. Trades run in supplied date order, preserving supplied order on the same day. Closing prices are explicit valuation assumptions. No price path, historical series or return process is inferred from trade prices. Unknown references, unresolved critical provenance, ID collisions and out-of-period trades block calculation.

FIFO orders by acquisition date; HIFO orders by federal basis per share, with stable date/ID tie breaks. Specific identification honors the supplied ordered lot list and rejects locked or mismatched lots. HIFO is a selection policy, not a determination that the broker accepted specific identification. Selection validates total availability before mutation. Partial sales allocate basis and net proceeds while retaining exact residual totals. Purchase commissions enter basis; sale commissions reduce proceeds. Purchases must be funded by that account's cash.

The UI reports pre-adjustment tax-basis gains, economic P&L when cost is supplied, remaining lots, cash, transaction costs and marked gross wealth. Original units sold and ending market-value weights are shown separately. Exposure groups aggregate configured long-only values; they do not infer ETF look-through, beta or derivative-adjusted economic exposure. It explicitly does not report household harvesting benefits or after-tax wealth. Retirement holdings, gains with different jurisdiction bases and loss trades can be inspected without inventing tax consequences.

## Implemented federal wash tracking

Optional `federal_wash_tracking` processes each trade before observing later trades. It matches same-security retained purchases in the 30 days before a loss and subsequent purchases through day 30, across all configured taxable and IRA/Roth accounts. Same-day purchases preserve supplied order. Partial replacements split quantities, all bases and economic cost; only matched taxable shares receive the federal basis adjustment. IRA/Roth replacements deny the matched federal loss without increasing IRA basis. Future holding periods include prior holding time, excluding the gap between sale and later replacement. Original-share reporting retains lot lineage through splits. Unsold shares from a recent acquisition can replace shares sold from that same purchase; grouping them under one import lot ID must not change recognition. A full exit leaves no shares that could replace themselves. This share-level interpretation follows the acquisition and matching rules in [26 CFR 1.1091-1(a), (c)–(e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.1091-1), checked October 23, 2026.

`opening_wash_context_complete` confirms all relevant retained household purchases are represented, opening bases are adjusted, and there are no unresolved pre-opening loss sales. Otherwise the case must start earlier. An unresolved forward window reports a pending loss rather than an available deduction. The closing date includes all supplied trades that day, so day 30 closes the window. Disposals remain assigned to their original sale dates.

Federal disallowed, basis-deferred, permanently denied and pending losses are separate fields. Economic cost is optional and independent of tax basis: if unknown, economic P&L remains unknown; a federal deferral never becomes a new economic loss. NJ gains remain unconfirmed by default; the explicit NJ scenario choices below enable conditional recognition. Cross-security matching/conversion ratios, controlled corporations, derivatives and full household tax attribution are not implemented here. Duplicate substantially-identical groups across different security IDs block enabled tracking rather than silently skipping the relation. This is an initial model under development, not a broker tax-reporting replacement.

## Implemented dated sale decisions

Each optional `sale_policies` entry supplies a date, account, security, lot priority, commission and a complete price snapshot for that decision. Manual orders run before policies on the same day; ties within each input list retain their order. The engine never substitutes closing prices or later decisions into an earlier decision. The UI provides a form for one additional decision, with repeated/staged dates available in the case JSON.

`retain` executes no sale. `sell_all` sells the selected account's currently liquid shares. `original_fraction` is a cumulative target against that account/security's opening shares: previous manual sales count, replacement purchases do not become original shares, and wash splits retain lineage. Repeating the same target does not sell the same fraction twice. Fractional quantities use six decimal places; original-share targets round down to avoid exceeding the requested cumulative fraction.

`target_weight` limits the security's gross market-value share of all configured accounts and cash. It accounts for the proposed sale commission in the denominator and rounds the requested quantity up to six decimals. It can sell only liquid shares in the authorized account, even when other accounts hold the same security. Results show requested, sold and unfilled units plus actual before/after weights. They expose an unattainable target instead of silently selling another account or locked shares. If sale commission exceeds available proceeds, no sale or fee occurs. Taxes are not included in these gross concentration targets; tax-budget decisions are implemented separately below; after-tax concentration targets remain pending.

All generated orders use the ordinary lot selection, cash and wash ledger. Executed orders are exported for inspection. Tests cover cumulative staging, commissions, household account limits, locked shares, same-day order, HIFO exclusion of new purchases, and invariance to future/closing price changes.

## NJ scenario treatment and independent basis

`nj_wash_treatment` defaults to `unconfirmed`: raw NJ basis gains remain visible, but recognized NJ income is unknown and annual tax comparisons are blocked. `federal_rules` explicitly applies the modeled federal stock/securities matching mechanics to the separate NJ loss amounts and basis. `no_wash_assumption` is an explicit counterfactual that recognizes raw NJ gains/losses without wash adjustments; it is not a validated statement of NJ law.

The same matching implementation serves both jurisdictions, but each has independent losses, replacement usage, deferred/denied amounts and pending windows. A federal gain may coexist with an NJ loss or vice versa. Partial splits retain purchase order and both ledgers' usage markers. Split IDs are never recycled after disposal. No jurisdiction's wash adjustment changes economic cost or the other jurisdiction's basis. Retirement-account sales are not distributions; IRA/Roth replacement-loss denial is conditional on the selected NJ mechanics.

[2025 NJ-1040 instructions, printed page 12](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/pdf/current/1040i.pdf) generally use federal cost/adjusted basis, identify exceptions, and direct taxpayers to list NJ-taxable Schedule D transactions. [NJ Capital Gains guidance, updated March 31, 2026](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/njit9.shtml) describes basis exceptions, realization-year reporting and lack of short/long-term differentiation. These support the general basis/category mapping. They do not explicitly resolve every spouse/IRA wash edge case checked here; the detailed NJ matching choice remains a disclosed modeling assumption. NJ-exempt obligations, business-interest basis adjustments and other special dispositions require separate validated mappings before being treated as supported ordinary taxable securities.

## Annual lot-tax comparisons

Optional `lot_tax_cases` supplies one annual filing-unit forecast per calendar year, in order. Each baseline contains other income, deductions and pre-opening activity, excluding the disposals and distributions modeled by this lot case. Federal wash tracking and an explicit NJ treatment are required. Only the first year supplies opening capital-loss carry; later years propagate the with-trade and without-trade carries independently. NJ disposition losses follow the NJ engine's same-category rules and do not become a federal-style capital carry.

Recognized short-term, long-term and collectibles gains retain their character. Pending losses receive no deduction. A January replacement revises the original December loss year, rather than creating a January loss. Results show both annual liabilities, their difference, pending amounts and remaining federal carry. Entered deductions are fixed in this comparison: it does not infer the payment-date SALT/refund feedback or subtract liability from portfolio cash. It is a conditional annual-liability comparison, not spendable terminal wealth or a household cash-flow comparison.

## Current-loss attribution

The annual UI separately reports current recognized portfolio losses, offsets against own gains and external net gains, ordinary-income consumption and attributed ending carry. This is an explicit reporting allocation, not a statutory identification of whose loss was used: net within short/long character first and then across characters; consume current portfolio losses before external net losses and opening carry; offset own gains before external gains. Capital-gain distributions count as own gains. External inputs are net amounts, so their unobserved gross transactions cannot be reconstructed.

Ordinary consumption is the residual after the existing annual engine's character-specific carry worksheet, including unused low-income deductions. Each character's recognized current loss equals own-gain use + external-gain use + ordinary consumption + attributed ending carry. Pending and disallowed losses never enter that sum. Earlier-year carry is shown as an opening amount; its historical origins are not relabeled as current-year losses. The total carry remains in the annual comparison.

An allocated offset can displace an existing carry rather than create incremental tax savings. Only the matched annual liability difference measures that conditional tax effect. Tests include full displacement with zero additional saving, mixed short/long losses, low-income unused deductions, distributions, future-year carry and unrecognized wash/retirement losses.

## Tax-budget decisions

`tax_budget` policies require the annual forecasts and an explicit nonnegative dollar budget. At the decision date, trial sales use the same selected lots, commissions, separate bases and wash ledgers as actual execution. Only trades already executed are observed. Full-year other-income forecasts are supplied assumptions; future trade prices/orders are unavailable to the decision. Pending wash losses cannot finance a tax benefit.

The budget limits the current year's modeled liability difference versus the no-modeled-lot-trades path, including each path's earlier carry consequences. It is cumulative: repeated policies cannot reuse a previously consumed annual budget. If earlier trades already exceed it and no evaluated sale repairs that, zero shares are sold and the diagnostic remains over budget. Tax liability is reported rather than paid from cash in this workbench. Subsequent manual trades or revised forecasts can invalidate the decision-date estimate.

The bounded search evaluates a configurable 2–128 interval grid (default 32), then refines above the largest feasible sample to microshare resolution or 24 iterations. Because credits, loss lots and tax cliffs can make liability nonmonotone, the result is the largest evaluated feasible quantity, not a certified global optimum. All reported executed quantities satisfy the evaluated budget unless the baseline was already over it; that condition is explicit.

## Matched harvesting and taxable exit

Each `harvest_policies` entry supplies dated prices, a taxable account, source and replacement securities, explicit replacement eligibility, loss thresholds, selection order, optional gross-sale cap and per-trade commission. Only liquid lots with a federal loss exceeding both thresholds qualify. Fees do not manufacture threshold losses. Replacement shares are rounded down to six decimals and funded solely by sale proceeds less both commissions; residual cash remains in the account. Every sale and purchase passes through the existing basis, cash and wash ledgers.

A distinct ticker is not legal proof of a permitted replacement: eligibility and distinct substantially-identical groups must be supplied. Known household loss sales of the replacement group in the prior 30 days block the replacement. Later manual purchases remain unknown at decision time and can still wash a harvested loss. Distribution entitlements, recognition and receipts precede fee assessments, then manual trades, sale policies and harvesting policies on the same date. Collectibles and retirement-account harvesting are rejected by this stock/securities policy.

`compare_harvesting` replays the same opening portfolio, manual trades, sale policies, dated prices and commission schedule without harvest policies. Actual transaction costs differ when trades differ. It reports marked wealth difference, annual filing-unit liability saving, their sum, and extra trading costs. Without annual tax forecasts, tax saving and tax-adjusted difference remain unknown. All annual amounts are undiscounted; tax savings are not paid into or reinvested by the portfolio. Replacement underperformance remains visible rather than being attributed to tax efficiency.

Optional `terminal_exit` sells liquid positions in selected taxable accounts at closing marks through the ordinary sale ledger, charging a commission per account/security. Locked positions remain visible. Retirement accounts cannot be selected. Losses whose forward wash windows are still open remain pending unless the user explicitly assumes no substantially-identical purchases for 30 days after exit. This view estimates incremental annual liability under a stated exit; it does not claim complete household spendable wealth.

The synthetic UI example harvests a $250 loss and shows a $55 annual liability reduction while held. With matched price recovery and a same-year exit, that reduction reverses to zero. Checks separately cover replacement underperformance, commissions, partial sale caps, locked positions, future wash purchases, pending exit losses, missing tax forecasts and invariance of prior decisions to closing prices.

## Recurring portfolio fees

`fee_schedules` distinguishes advisor, manager, custody and other charges. Each schedule specifies valuation accounts, optional security scope, whether cash is included, annual rate, fixed amount per period, day-count denominator, paying account and explicit dated valuation periods. Each period supplies its own complete price snapshot and may override the calculated base with an explicit contractual amount. Periods must be ordered and nonoverlapping; contiguous periods share a boundary. The proportional charge is ending-snapshot base × annual rate × elapsed calendar days / day-count denominator, rounded to cents, plus the fixed amount. This does not infer an average-daily-value or beginning-value contract.

All assessments on the same date use the same pre-payment cash snapshot, so ordering advisor and custody schedules cannot change cash-inclusive bases. Assessments precede that day's trades and policy decisions. Cash charges consume available cash only in the specified paying account; no sale or borrowing is invented. Unpaid amounts remain liabilities. Later sale proceeds settle those obligations before reinvestment. Harvest replacements reserve any existing fee debt before allocating sale proceeds. Terminal exits settle the same liabilities without charging the expense twice.

`embedded_in_prices` records a fee estimate for disclosure but changes neither cash nor wealth: supplied prices must already reflect it. Cash fees have no automatic tax deduction. The UI reports assessed, paid, owed and embedded amounts separately, with each valuation base and its source. The legacy `gross_wealth` output means marked wealth before income tax, after paid fees and remaining fee liabilities; it is not gross asset value or household spendable wealth. Concentration targets use that same pre-tax denominator.

Matched harvesting runs share schedules; their actual fees can differ when they own different sleeves or have different valuation bases. The synthetic manager example assesses a 1% annual fee on the replacement ETF for 183/365 of a year: $5.01 on its $1,000 assessment value, zero on the unowned sleeve in the harvesting-off run. It changes the $55 conditional holding-period tax difference to $49.99 after the extra fee. This is an explicit synthetic contract, not a quoted product fee.

## Distribution entitlement, receipts and tax character

`distributions` supplies a security, ex-date, payment date, recognition date, per-share amount and independent federal/NJ character. Ex-date entitlements are frozen before that day's trades. A later sale retains its earned payment; an ex-date purchase earns none. Unpaid entitlements remain receivables, never purchase funding. Per-account cumulative rounding preserves total cash across fractional lots. Payment converts receivable to cash and settles existing fee obligations. Explicit recognition dates support a different tax year from cash receipt; they are assumptions requiring the applicable rule to be established.

Ordinary dividends, interest, non-AMT exempt interest and ordinary long-term capital-gain distributions map into the annual engine. Retirement receipts stay inside their wrapper without becoming withdrawals. Baseline annual forecasts exclude modeled distributions. The legacy with/without-lot-trades output keys now compare all modeled portfolio income events; UI labels say portfolio events. No automatic reinvestment occurs: an explicit purchase uses received cash and the ordinary commission/wash ledger. Price-only marks must be confirmed to avoid adding cash distributions to an already reinvested total-return series.

For common stock/fund dividends, the supplied potential qualified fraction requires confirmed issuer eligibility and undiminished risk. Actual holding days exclude acquisition day and include disposal day within the 121-day ex-date window; more than 60 are required. Stable purchase/offset ranges track partial sales and wash splits. Wash-tacked capital-gain holding periods do not replace actual acquisition dates for this check. Future holding days stay pending and receive ordinary treatment until observed; tax-budget trials use their decision date, not the projection end. Preferred-share periods and partially hedged holding days need separate models.

[IRS Publication 550 (2025), dividends and other distributions](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550) supports the character and holding-period distinctions, January-payment special rule, and separate fund-loss rules. Return-of-capital/basis changes, liquidation distributions, substitute payments, private-activity AMT interest and special short-held fund-loss adjustments are not yet implemented. A conservative guard rejects an overlapping loss on shares held at most 184 days after an actual capital-gain/exempt-interest fund entitlement; it does not apply future distributions or other purchases' entitlements. These exclusions remain unfinished scope, not certified zero-tax cases.

## Next implementation constraints

Wash matching must consider the entire configured household, including spouse and IRA/Roth purchases, with explicit substantially-identical groups. Groups and replacement eligibility are assumptions; the program cannot infer legal identity from different tickers. Preserve share-level partial matches, replacement basis adjustments and holding-period carryover; IRA replacements must not receive a taxable-account basis increase. Track unresolved future wash windows without giving a decision policy access to future purchases or returns. A December loss and January replacement require original-year recognition, not a January capital loss.

Policies must distinguish fractions of original shares, portfolio-value concentration targets and economic exposure. Tax-budget decisions already use the annual filing-unit engine and available information; household cash funding still needs actual-lot integration. A matched harvesting-off run shares prices, cash flows and fees. Gross losses, disallowed/deferred losses, recognized losses, own-gain use, external-gain offsets, carry and terminal liquidation must remain separate. Account sales that fund household obligations will eventually select actual lots instead of the tax layer's interim proportional-basis adapter; the two adapters must never dispose of the same account twice.
