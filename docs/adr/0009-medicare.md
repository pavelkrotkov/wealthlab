# ADR 0009: Medicare income lookback and premium funding

Status: Medicare cash layer of retirement ticket #6. [Direct-billed household integration](0017-household-medicare.md) now uses completed tax history and shared healthcare funding. Benefit withholding, SMI rounding and other documented gaps remain open.

The workbench accepts confirmed whole-month enrollment with exclusive end dates. Enrollment is never inferred from age or retirement. Part A premiums, drug-plan premiums and other health costs are supplied in 2026 dollars. Full Part B and Part D income-related amounts use the published 2026 schedules. A user must confirm standard full Part B applicability; hold-harmless and immunosuppressive-only coverage are not silently charged the standard rate. Plan premiums cannot be charged for parts without enrollment. Coverage periods cannot overlap for one person; adjacent periods can change costs.

An income record is a tax return shared by its listed Medicare members. Joint-return MAGI is not divided between spouses. Individual returns cannot be shared by two people. MAGI is supplied AGI plus tax-exempt interest; negative AGI remains possible. Premium-year income normally comes from two years earlier, with no automatic selection of the current year's lower retirement income. Missing history blocks calculation. An explicit three-year fallback requires absence of the normal two-year record. A conditional SSA redetermination can instead reference prior/current income, but does not assert that SSA approved an appeal or that the life event qualifies. The decision belongs to the named person and premium year, not automatically both spouses.

The 2026 intermediate brackets change only above their upper boundaries; the final bracket starts at its threshold. Married-filing-separately persons who lived together have separate $109,000/$391,000 boundaries. Supplied full-year lived-apart status uses the individual schedule. The engine does not determine residency facts or submit the individual attestation SSA requires for lived-apart relief. Part B and D surcharges apply only to the parts in which the person is enrolled. Part D's plan premium is separate from its income-related surcharge.

Later years use an explicitly frozen-2026 or indexed-2026 scenario. In the latter, premium and threshold growth are separate inputs and all supplied costs are scaled from 2026 dollars. Neither is represented as published future law, and the result labels each year's rule mode. Decimal calculations reject growth beyond the supported monetary range. No host-current-year default changes a saved case.

Costs accrue on the first of the coverage month. This is an explicit planning convention, not a claim about actual Medicare billing dates or the date Social Security withholds premiums. Same-day cash receipts arrive before bills; supplied coverage order breaks same-day funding ties. Available cash pays oldest bills first. Shortfalls become outstanding costs rather than hidden loans or negative cash. Later receipts can pay those arrears, and the payment ledger retains the actual modeled payment date and original bill ID. Each cost also retains its amount unfunded when due, so later curing does not erase a spending failure. Coverage continues as supplied during the stress; cancellation or reinstatement is not predicted.

The workbench neither withdraws from an HSA nor duplicates a household spending payment. Later integration must coordinate eligibility, premium withholding, taxable benefits, HSA reimbursement and medical deduction payment dates against this ledger. Other health costs remain supplied estimates. Late-enrollment penalties, subsidies, Extra Help, Medicare Advantage reductions, hold-harmless, specialized Part B, actual billing and household annual-tax integration remain unfinished. No tax saving is calculated on this page.
