# ADR 0062: Dated QOZ source gains and actual fund cash

Status: bounded same-source-year consumer for [issue #12](https://github.com/pavelkrotkov/wealthlab/issues/12). Cross-year investments remain blocked pending original-return reconciliation. No dependency added.

## Reuse and ownership

`Scenario.qoz_plan` adds a QOF **C-corporation stock** profile to the existing composite product ledger. One existing long/short economic book with zero shorts owns the corporation assets, loans and cash; the existing engine calculates entered leverage, financing, manager fees, transaction costs, internal maintenance and liquidation once. Each actual call adds stock-lot units, not a second creditor book. Normal subscriptions use pre-call NAV pricing; new cash first repays existing debt, then the existing rebalance helper resets the whole company to the supplied target exposures. NAV pricing, common stock and retargeting on calls are explicit contract assumptions. Stock lots keep separate tax clocks/bases and share the same later return. Largest-remainder cent allocation preserves each actual distribution, stock FMV and sale-proceeds total. Returns explicitly include entity-level tax before these separately modeled costs. Limited liability contains internal deficits; debt does not become shareholder basis or a household bill.

Calls represent explicitly confirmed optional subscriptions accepted up to actual available cash, without binding unfunded commitments, default penalties or forfeiture remedies. Those terms require another profile. Calls occur after actual linked stock sales and prior tax arrears, before current taxes/spending. A call identifies its executed trade and disposed lot. Nonpositive-NAV recapitalizations need supplied issuance units (in the initial one-unit-per-dollar scale) and explicit terms. Cash can cure existing creditor deficits without creating a fresh NAV book. This bounded recapitalization profile requires no actual QOZ elections; continuing or new QOZ tax benefits need another supported tax profile. The supported common-stock terms explicitly assume subscriptions participate in declared dividends on their funding date: post-contribution NAV sets the dividend amount and units divide it among all stock lots. This is a supplied cum-dividend entitlement assumption, not a claim that new cash earned a pre-subscription return. Different record-date or ex-dividend subscription terms require another profile. Subscriptions during a market lock require a pending-investment profile and are rejected here. Elected gain is capped by actual cash invested and unused positive source gain; original ST/LT character survives. Source sale proceeds enter cash once through the lot engine. QOZ exclusions and inclusions have zero cash. Insufficient calls report actual unfunded amounts, rather than borrowing against prospective savings.

## Conditional stock tax and liquidity

The investment date selects legacy December 31, 2026 inclusion or post-2026 five-year inclusion. Sale-date clocks use the 180-day period beginning on the actual direct sale, including that day. Every tranche keeps a separate qualifying/nonqualifying basis in each jurisdiction. The five-year increment precedes inclusion; the independently supplied rural assumption changes 10% to 30%. Inclusion uses the lesser of elected gain and qualifying FMV, less applicable basis. A full stock-interest sale first includes remaining original gain, then recognizes stock appreciation. The optional ten-year FMV election applies only to appreciated qualifying stock; normal-cost shares remain taxable. Input preflight rejects a requested legacy election whose first modeled executable sale falls after 2047, including a dealing lock delaying a 2047 request. The runtime guard remains defense in depth. Legacy post-2047 election continuation is not inferred from the expiring designation safe harbor and requires another profile. Post-2026 thirty-year-cap observations are rejected when the actual anniversary lies inside the exclusive horizon.

Only confirmed nonqualified ordinary earnings-and-profits cash dividends are supported; qualified dividends need independently classified inputs in another profile. They reduce actual NAV and create taxable ordinary dividends, without inventing a return-of-capital or debt-funded-distribution classification. Declared E&P dividends have no investor stock-sale cost; the configured exit-cost rate applies only to the final stock-interest sale. Entered preferred return is a simple actual-day/365 carry hurdle, with no catch-up or guaranteed payment. At final sale, pooled carry applies to positive sale cash after selling costs plus prior dividends above total contributed capital and the contribution-date-weighted hurdle. Selling costs reduce actual proceeds. Tax is recognized on the stock sale, before delayed cash settlement. The settlement receivable remains wealth and cannot fund taxes or spending; it deposits once.

Whole calendar months are required; month-end calls earn no earlier return. Distributions precede that date's ex-dividend FMV; sale carry precedes sale inclusion. Exact statutory inclusion FMV must be observed: unsupported February/leap clocks fail. Opening QOF interests, pass-through/1231/collectible clocks, prior-QOF redeferrals, transfers, return-of-capital/extraordinary distributions, partial stock sales and thirty-year-cap exits need separate profiles. No universal QOZ qualification or partnership K-1/basis model is claimed.

Products / **Opportunity-zone funds** edits the actual household and contract. Denied, deferral-only and conditional ten-year federal policies share identical economic terms/returns. NJ has independently supplied gain/basis and limits, while federal denial cannot grant an unsupported NJ-only election. A separate liquid reference explicitly removes leverage, fees and carry and settles at the sale request; it uses the same entered after-entity-tax return series, not a claim of comparable pre-tax corporate/ETF returns. Unsettled NAV/claims prevent a fully liquidated ranking. Full inputs, sources, source disposals, calls, economic/tax/basis audits and outcomes export together. Annual income-tax rates remain explicit hypothetical policy inputs.

[NJ's February 4, 2026 statement](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/federaltaxcuts.shtml) follows federal QOZ timing/election for individuals; the independently supplied state profile still requires confirmation of post-2026 treatment. No sponsor terms are verified by these authorities.

## Checks

Independent examples conserve original stock/cash capital through calls, leverage/interest/fees, cash dividends, carry and delayed settlement. A $50,000 recapitalization cures an existing $50,000 creditor deficit to zero NAV; no new call leaves that debt inside the company without taking shareholder cash. A partial $20,000 cure leaves $30,000 entity debt. Different-date lots share one later leveraged return and one interest/fee stream. Pooled carry weights actual contribution dates and uses after-selling-cost cash. Qualified recapitalizations and unsupported clocks fail closed. Actual household tests cover source identity, the 180th day, repeated-call caps, funding gaps, no investor recourse, mandatory inclusion without cash, denied/deferred/allowed economics and simultaneous exchange/QOZ ledgers. The real Streamlit form submits editable inputs, displays cash/tax results, clears invalid stale output and retains all existing product routes.
