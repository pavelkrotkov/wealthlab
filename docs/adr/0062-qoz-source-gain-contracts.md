# ADR 0062: Dated QOZ source gains and actual fund cash

Status: bounded same-source-year consumer for [issue #12](https://github.com/pavelkrotkov/wealthlab/issues/12). Cross-year investments remain blocked pending original-return reconciliation. No dependency added.

## Reuse and ownership

`Scenario.qoz_plan` adds a QOF **C-corporation stock** profile to the existing composite product ledger. Each actual funded cash call opens the existing long/short economic book with zero shorts; the existing engine calculates entered leverage, financing, manager fees, transaction costs, internal maintenance and liquidation. Returns explicitly include entity-level tax before these separately modeled costs. Limited liability contains internal deficits; debt does not become shareholder basis or a household bill.

Calls occur after actual linked stock sales and prior tax arrears, before current taxes/spending. A call identifies its executed trade and disposed lot. Elected gain is capped by actual cash invested and unused positive source gain; original ST/LT character survives. Source sale proceeds enter cash once through the lot engine. QOZ exclusions and inclusions have zero cash. Insufficient calls report actual unfunded amounts, rather than borrowing against prospective savings.

## Conditional stock tax and liquidity

The investment date selects legacy December 31, 2026 inclusion or post-2026 five-year inclusion. Sale-date clocks use the 180-day period beginning on the actual direct sale, including that day. Every tranche keeps a separate qualifying/nonqualifying basis in each jurisdiction. The five-year increment precedes inclusion; the independently supplied rural assumption changes 10% to 30%. Inclusion uses the lesser of elected gain and qualifying FMV, less applicable basis. A full stock-interest sale first includes remaining original gain, then recognizes stock appreciation. The optional ten-year FMV election applies only to appreciated qualifying stock; normal-cost shares remain taxable.

Only confirmed ordinary earnings-and-profits cash dividends are supported. They reduce actual NAV and create taxable ordinary dividends, without inventing a return-of-capital or debt-funded-distribution classification. Entered preferred return is a simple actual-day/365 carry hurdle, with no catch-up or guaranteed payment. At final sale, carry applies to positive total proceeds plus prior dividends above contributed capital and the hurdle. Selling costs reduce actual proceeds. Tax is recognized on the stock sale, before delayed cash settlement. The settlement receivable remains wealth and cannot fund taxes or spending; it deposits once.

Month-end calls earn no earlier return. Distributions precede that date's ex-dividend FMV; sale carry precedes sale inclusion. Exact statutory inclusion FMV must be observed: unsupported February/leap clocks fail. Opening QOF interests, pass-through/1231/collectible clocks, prior-QOF redeferrals, transfers, return-of-capital/extraordinary distributions, partial stock sales and thirty-year-cap exits need separate profiles. No universal QOZ qualification or partnership K-1/basis model is claimed.

Products / **Opportunity-zone funds** edits the actual household and contract. Denied, deferral-only and conditional ten-year federal policies share identical economic terms/returns. NJ has independently supplied gain/basis and limits, while federal denial cannot grant an unsupported NJ-only election. A separate liquid reference explicitly removes leverage, fees and carry and settles at the sale request; it uses the same entered after-entity-tax return series, not a claim of comparable pre-tax corporate/ETF returns. Unsettled NAV/claims prevent a fully liquidated ranking. Full inputs, sources, source disposals, calls, economic/tax/basis audits and outcomes export together. Annual income-tax rates remain explicit hypothetical policy inputs.

## Primary evidence checked October 25, 2026

Local tax-reference discovery returned older Schedule D material, useful for legacy terminology but insufficient for the new transition. The [current statute and Amendment of Section](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t26/s1400Z-2/) supply the investment-date effective rule, clocks, basis ordering, mixed investments and ten-/thirty-year tests. [Treasury regulation §1.1400Z2(b)-1(c)(8), (d), (e), (g)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.1400Z2(b)-1) distinguishes C-corporation distributions, holding periods, inclusion and basis. [Notice 2026-40 §§4.01–4.03](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-26-40.pdf) announces anticipated transition regulations: pre-2027 gains can be timely invested after 2026, while mandatory legacy deemed inclusion is not a fresh gain for re-election. It is not described as final transition regulation. [NJ's February 4, 2026 statement](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/federaltaxcuts.shtml) follows federal QOZ timing/election for individuals; the independently supplied state profile still requires confirmation of post-2026 treatment. No sponsor terms are verified by these authorities.
