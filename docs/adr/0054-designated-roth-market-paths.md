# 0054: Actual designated Roth cash consumes realized plan marks and locks

Status: accepted. Depends on [ordinary designated Roth plan cash](0053-designated-roth-plan-cash.md).

Markets / Household funding adds **Designated Roth plan cash**. The existing
four path methods supply actual month-end plan returns and outgoing liquidity
restrictions to the same complete-contract cost ledger. A prior market loss
can eliminate taxable earnings without clipping the opening cost or creating
a deduction. A later gain cannot finance an earlier cash request. Funded Roth
payroll and ordinary cash retain the workplace chronology from ADR0053.

Per-path results show actual gross cash, unfilled optional requests, first
unfilled date and separate federal/conditional NJ taxable amounts. They remain
separate from mandatory household spending failure. Selected-path details show
the contract value, remaining independent cost, participation clock and source
payment audit. Existing shared-batch policy comparisons and input/result exports
remain available without a new simulation or tax algorithm.

Six new checks run all four path methods, whole-horizon illiquidity and the
submitted Markets form. Across paths, original$10k assets plus actual inside-plan
investment change minus reported tax liability equals nominal ending wealth.
The selected stress uses actual source locks: a blocked$5k optional request is
reported once, remaining federal$9.4k cost is preserved, and no taxable cash is
invented. Two existing workplace return/cash regressions also pass.

Federal ordinary source eligibility and the independently entered conditional
NJ qualification/pro-rata profile remain explicit. Frozen2026 rules, complete
contracts, separation/access, excluded special histories and the absence of
modeled cash-loss deductions remain those of ADR0053. This two-year synthetic
factor proxy is not a lifetime funding result, security-specific allocation,
complete after-tax liquidation value or rollover recommendation. Retirement,
rebalancing and bounded policy optimization tickets remain open.
