# Native product exits in conditional policy search

Retirement, taxable investment, spending and account policies can now run alongside fixed long/short, private-fund, exchange-fund and life-policy profiles. Every candidate retains the same product capital, contract, independently supplied tax allocations, basis assumptions and insurance illustration. This layer does not optimize product allocations or infer a replacement illustration after changing premiums. Those controls, employer/options integration and cross-border optimization remain required for issue18.

## Actual exit evidence

A zero account balance cannot prove that a product has closed. The existing native books now expose terminal evidence, which the optimizer checks in addition to wallet balances and complete, resolved annual tax years:

- Margin sleeves require actual closure, zero gross long and short positions, zero segregated short collateral, zero free product cash and no financing loan. The current household wallet is not another product asset.
- A confirmed limited-liability private fund requires its actual final investor liquidation, no outstanding settlement records, no outside basis and no deferred recognition events. Internal fund leverage is separate from investor obligations under the supplied no-calls contract; its raw debt is not silently made recourse to the household.
- Exchange funds require an inactive interest, zero NAV and product cash/fees, no remaining source or basket shares, and no unrecognized future tax events. An active zero-NAV interest is still open. Native contribution, release and sale accounting remains unchanged.
- Life policies require the executed book to be closed, with no remaining policy loan or net cash, surrender or death value. A zero-valued in-force policy is not a surrender. Native loan settlement and federal/independent NJ recognition remain authoritative.

The sleeve evidence is appended to the final existing audit row for each account. Exchange evidence is included in its existing audit. No duplicate wallet or tax engine was introduced. Supplied sleeve allocations and private-fund deferred exit losses remain visible after economic closure; recognition beyond the projection horizon prevents an after-tax exit ranking.

## Common paths and stated insurance needs

Every optimized sleeve must bind to the shared realized market batch. The household market compiler also reuses the existing exchange price adapter, with an explicit security-to-factor mapping and replacement confirmation. Source and basket prices use the same selected path, with no additional random draw. Only price-plus-cash convention with zero cash yield on mapped exchange factors is supported: product distributions and tax allocations are independently supplied. Positive path yield is rejected rather than discarded or counted twice. Supplied life-policy values stay conditional and do not acquire an invented equity return. Product issuer inventories remain unmeasured where no look-through holdings exist; the concentration constraint is not waived automatically.

Common market product feasibility now includes required life-payment failures and the separately entered death-protection shortfall. Actual surrender can leave the household solvent while failing its protection requirement. These failures and dates are exposed in outcomes and affect joint funding/product safety. An optimizer cannot silently set the requirement to zero.

## Synthetic UI and validation

Optimize offers an optional product starter that composes with the existing account, diversification and robustness starters. It explicitly adds $10,000 of opening synthetic capital for each of four products. The exchange uses an independent price-only factor, the short illustrative contract has a supplied 2027 liquidity date, and historical exchange observations are invented zeros. Those assumptions are not commercial terms, observed history or tax eligibility. Its insurance need is explicitly zero; entering a positive need preserves that requirement. Default age100/95%/two-year reserve and concentration targets stay enabled.

A flat-price, zero-product-cost, full-basis check reconciles $340,000 opening wealth less $48,000 spending and $1 ordinary brokerage commission to $291,999 actual ending cash. Private redemption, ordinary sleeve liquidation, exchange basket sale and policy surrender return their respective $10,000 once. The frozen held-out policy reaches the same independently calculated result. Separate native cases retain future deferred tax, unpaid fund settlement and zero-valued in-force contracts as incomplete exits; a stated insurance need fails safety after surrender. Checks also cover subcent gross obligations, missing evidence, complete market mapping, unsupported yield and native UI audit export.
