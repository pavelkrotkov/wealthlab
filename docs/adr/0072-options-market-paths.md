# ADR 0072: Bounded option execution on shared realized paths

Status: dependent market layer for issue #10, after the dated contract and actual household ledgers in ADR0070/0071. Unsupported periods and contracts are guarded, rather than priced with invented quotes.

`HouseholdBinding.options` maps the complete option account to two distinct explicit stock/index factors. It supplies fixed implied volatilities, risk-free rate and continuous pricing yields separately from the underlying return process's volatility and cash yield. The user confirms replacement of entered marks and hypothetical model execution. Price-only monthly factors compound opening spots at eight-decimal precision. The compiler replaces every mark with the opening date plus month ends, preserving original inputs and the shared batch/index identity for reproduction. Before household execution, independently reconstructed marks must match that exact realized path; stale prices cannot enter a different path.

This first profile supports contracts opened at the household start, in-horizon expiries on month ends, explicitly supplied month-end exercise scenarios and a hypothetical complete exit. A future expiry can remain outstanding at the horizon or close at the explicitly elected model-priced terminal unwind. Future entries and rolls are rejected because fixed premiums/closing quotes cannot be reused after path prices change. Intramonth expiry, exercise, dividend entitlement/payment and arbitrary close quotes need additional dated observations and execution adapters. American exercise style does not infer actual assignment decisions. Liquidity locks on either bound factor are rejected until delivery obligations and a resolution policy can be modeled. Complete write-off or a spot that rounds to zero is also unsupported.

Generated cash yields on bound factors must be zero; actual dividends use explicit dated entitlements and receipts under the existing independently declared ordinary-dividend tax profile. Continuous option-pricing yields do not deposit cash or certify discrete-dividend exercise values. Historical replay supplies only underlying observations. The resulting option marks remain counterfactual QuantLib values under the submitted valuation assumptions, never historical option quotes or verified manager performance. Actual quote availability, implied-volatility surfaces, bid/ask spreads and additional broker terms are not fabricated.

The persistent household option book consumes actual finite cash, reserves collateral, executes actual strike delivery against the existing share/basis book and produces zero-cash federal/NJ recognition for the ordinary annual engine. Existing tax payment, spending, arrears and loan rules therefore observe the same account changes. All observed marks, including the opening mark before month end, participate in option feasibility. Funded collateral calls stay distinct from unresolved shortfalls. A later price recovery cannot erase an earlier unmet requirement. Negative equity, incomplete exits and failed execution join existing sleeve constraints in the shared `product_feasible`, `joint_feasible` and first-failure results. Cash funding and product feasibility remain separate.

Markets / Household funding / **Options household** offers independently synthetic covered-call, collar and four-leg examples in deterministic, Monte Carlo, invented historical replay and named stress modes. Paired policies use the same immutable batch and selected indices, with actual reported liability, tax cash, spending shortfalls and terminal wealth differences. Underlying factors price the options but do not establish derivative-adjusted issuer concentration; that coverage remains explicitly incomplete. One protective stock put cannot establish a floor on an unrelated index liability or on aggregate spendable wealth.

Independent flat-path checks reconcile the original zero-fee examples to $24,600 covered-call, $24,200 collar and $23,800 four-leg ending cash after $6,000 spending, starting from $20,000 cash plus 100 shares worth $10,000. Other checks cover a severe independent index loss/collateral shortfall despite the stock put, a resolved month-end margin balance with an earlier opening failure, ordinary dividend cash once, prior-mark invariance to later shocks, all market modes, precision failures, unavailable quote dates/locks, stale bindings and the real Markets form. These outcomes are conditional synthetic checks, not an investment recommendation or retirement certificate.
