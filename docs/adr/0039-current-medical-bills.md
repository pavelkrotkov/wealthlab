# ADR 0039: Current medical bills, HSA provider payments and actual cash fallback

Status: another vertical layer of open retirement ticket #6. The existing historical receipt and cash-HSA tax engines are reused. This layer pays net current medical bills, carries unpaid liabilities and creates reimbursement capacity only from actual cash payments. HSA investments/state basis, automatic medical deductions, insurance recoveries and broader workplace/benefit coverage remain unfinished.

## Declared bills and funding order

An optional `medical_bills` list in the household HSA plan supplies each bill's care date, due date, net household amount, provenance, declared medical eligibility and eligible HSA owners. A bill is a liability, not a historical paid receipt. Care cannot follow the due date; bills must fall inside the projection, amounts are positive, IDs remain globally unique, sources must identify known HSAs and unresolved eligibility blocks calculation. A selected HSA source and requested direct-payment cap are optional; a positive cap requires a source and cannot exceed the bill. Zero requests leave ordinary cash responsible.

The plan must confirm bills are already net of insurance/other reimbursement and excluded from other budgets, paid receipt inputs and deductions. Actual cash-paid portions are deliberately left undeducted so their paid receipt capacity can be preserved. This layer does not automatically claim federal/NJ medical deductions or infer whether cash reimbursement is preferable. Unrelated supplied deductions may coexist under the explicit exclusion confirmation. Future insurance recovery and medical deduction/recovery policies require separate accounting, not a second copy of a bill.

Existing dated HSA interest, contributions and withdrawals retain their chronology. On a shared day, existing withdrawals precede direct bill elections. A bill's direct HSA payment uses only assets available on that date and the remaining elected cap. After the month's dated HSA events, an explicit month-end retry can pay outstanding bills using later available HSA assets; it does not retrospectively create an earlier distribution. The existing household monthly budget pools ordinary cash receipts and does not certify intramonth cash liquidity.

Direct HSA payments go to providers. The HSA asset falls once; no matching household cash deposit is made. Qualified expense capacity, beneficiary/establishment conditions and nonqualified additional-tax exceptions come from the existing HSA engine. Nonqualified provider elections require the same explicit ordinary/noninvestment assumption, independent NJ fraction and duplicate-tax confirmation as nonqualified household cash distributions. Fractions apply to the whole actual funded distribution. Actual payment dates control age/disability exceptions. Requested but unfunded amounts do not create tax.

The remaining bill and prior arrears enter the permitted actual-lot funding quote. In the healthcare slot, Medicare collection remains first, then medical bills in due-date order, then ordinary healthcare budgets. Only actual cash paid creates new undeducted receipt capacity. A scheduled HSA withdrawal earlier in that month cannot consume cash paid later in the healthcare slot. The existing global receipt balance prevents reuse across owners, sources and years. A provider-paid portion is already satisfied and never becomes another reimbursement capacity.

## Liabilities and diagnostics

Unpaid bills remain separate `medical_owed` liabilities deducted once from net wealth. This is supplied net provider debt with no interest or late fees unless those are separately entered; no financing term is invented. Current new unpaid bills contribute once to monetary spending shortfall. Outstanding arrears keep monthly funding failure true without repeatedly counting the original cost. Paying old arrears reduces assets and liabilities together and is removed from recurring reserve demand.

The audit separates actual provider distributions, actual cash payments, remaining bill liabilities and requested/funded/unfilled direct HSA elections. An unfilled optional HSA election can coexist with a fully cash-paid bill; it is not itself a household funding failure. The ordinary HSA withdrawal-cash field excludes provider payments, while qualified/nonqualified tax totals include actual distributions of either destination. Annual receipt audits are captured after December cash spending; year boundaries carry the actual shared remaining balance.

## Executed synthetic checks

The UI example opens with USD1,000 cash and USD3,000 HSA. A USD6,000 January bill elects up to USD5,000 direct HSA funding. Actual provider payment is USD3,000 and cash payment USD1,000; the remaining USD2,000 liability makes net wealth negative USD2,000 and exposes a real failure. A supplied USD3,000 nonincome gift in February cures the USD2,000 arrears, leaves USD1,000 cash and creates no recurring reserve demand. A USD5,000 March employer HSA deposit later reimburses only the actual USD3,000 cash-paid portions. The following-year repeated claim cannot reuse the receipt. Ending assets are USD4,000 cash plus USD2,000 HSA: opening4,000 + gift3,000 + employer5,000 − bill6,000 = USD6,000. Taxes in this explicitly conditional low-income synthetic case are zero.

Other checks cover a persistent unfunded bill with no double cost, a later HSA deposit paid at the actual month-end retry, care predating establishment, two owners exhausting a single actual cash-paid receipt, December payment before annual receipt capture, and actual HIFO lot funding of the cash remainder. In the lot case, net proceeds USD2,000 plus opening cash and actual HSA provider funding pay the full bill; a USD1 commission reconciles wealth. A USD1,000 nonqualified provider payment creates USD1,000 ordinary income, USD200 additional federal tax and supplied USD500 NJ income; it never creates a household cash receipt. Real UI checks execute the new example and preserve both prior HSA profiles.
