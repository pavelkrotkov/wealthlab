# ADR 0141: Published common-regime Spanish regional policy

Status: implemented as the regional child of the conditional annual Spain assessment in issue #16. This replaces ADR0140's initial three-region implementation boundary. No new dependency or wallet engine.

The editable annual UI now offers all 15 common-regime regions. `spain_regions_2025.json` stores native floor/quota/rate rows, ordinary wealth minima, under-65/no-dependent synthetic personal minima and official source URLs. The existing printed Madrid/Catalonia/Castilla-La Mancha rows remain shared. Results retain the actual region, effective policy year, verification date, source URLs and applied rows. Future-year cases remain explicitly frozen-2025 illustrations.

## Independently supplied eligibility

The synthetic personal minima are examples for the stated assumptions; an actual person still supplies eligible state/regional personal minima and their source. The ordinary IP minimum is regional. An optional `wealth_allowance` retains an independently supplied eligible special minimum and its determination source. Only published amounts for that region are supported: Andalucía's additional EUR1250000/EUR1500000 and Extremadura's EUR600000/EUR700000/EUR800000. An unsupported amount blocks wealth, preserving an independently resolved income assessment. Selecting an amount does not establish disability, administrative recognition or other qualification.

Special protected-asset, forest, disability and cultural deductions remain independently prelimited `wealth_regional` relief with a source. No asset label automatically creates an entitlement. Navarra/Basque foral profiles, Ceuta/Melilla, joint aggregation, nonresident and special-regime calculations remain explicit implementation boundaries. The program does not infer a region from a move date or a US product label.

## Regional bonus and payment order

Andalucía, Madrid, Murcia and La Rioja use the temporary positive difference between capped IP and capped solidarity quotas, rather than the older general 100% rule. Cantabria uses full minor-quota relief for the supported low-wealth/no-solidarity case, otherwise the positive difference. Extremadura has its separately published 100% minor-quota bonus. Each computed bonus is retained separately from prelimited supplied credits and actual IP payment observations. An unpaid assessment still creates no solidarity credit.

Galicia is deliberately conditional. Its 50% minor-quota relief is reduced by solidarity payable; exhaustion can also reduce other regional deductions. Neither a fixed-point solver nor `max(IP, solidarity)` follows from the available procedural evidence. The caller must supply final total regional relief after applicable solidarity offsets and confirm `credits.regional_bonus_included_confirmed`, with its source. Unknown relief blocks only wealth and the combined total; IRPF remains visible. A supplied zero is an explicit prelimited assumption, not an automatic eligibility result or an inferred optimum.

## Reproducible evidence and integration seam

Independent regional-boundary checks assert printed accumulated quotas for every newly added income table, including late-2025 values. Separate EUR4m synthetic wealth cases hand-calculate the Balearic, Valencia and Asturias schedules; bonus cases distinguish full relief, replacement relief, unpaid assessments and blocked/supplied Galicia relief. Real Streamlit checks select Valencia and Galicia and verify the actual assessment/blocked result, export and combined-total availability.

The multi-year/person assessment adapter belongs above this regional layer. It will emit stable assessment and scheduled obligation identifiers from explicitly classified annual facts and observed payments. Issue #17 owns the single foreign-tax cash/refund/funding/credit book; this layer does not debit another wallet or turn an assessment into spendable cash.
