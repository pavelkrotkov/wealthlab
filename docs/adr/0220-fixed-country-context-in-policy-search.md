# Fixed country context in native policy search

October 27, 2026. The country is a shared scenario assumption, not an optimizer decision.

Reuse `jurisdiction_execution.evaluate` on every compiled candidate and realized market path. Each reciprocal country-tax pass starts a fresh native market execution over the same batch: CPI, guardrails, locks and month indices never carry across passes. Training, frozen validation and named checks use the same route. The existing native PR/Spain engines, original household wallet and foreign payment/credit book remain authoritative. Projection-work budgets include the native reconciliation bound.

`PolicySearch.country_context` retains an optional complete native `SpainExecution`, source availability/validity and explicit monthly wage links. PR residency/source profiles stay in the original annual tax cases. Every projected wage links its original household income ID to an existing person/year/source ID and month-end recognition date. Existing employment splitting and calendar accrual determine actual USD wages after retirement or a realized job change; the same amount updates the matching native US credit, PR and ordinary Spanish records. Opening income, source classification, local eligibility and independent FX stay unchanged. An explicit future-wage replacement confirmation makes those bound future amounts follow the native wage assumption, so editing projected pay does not require editing duplicate future tax rows. Historical source amounts and opening income are never rewritten.

This first mapping supports USD W2 wages with equal US/PR wage bases and separately supplied Spanish USD-to-EUR receipt quotes. It does not infer taxable wages after workplace/HSA deferrals, new retirement/product income classification, foreign capital-gain/source/basis mappings, treaty benefits, relocation eligibility or fiscal wealth values from market balances. Existing native reconciliation rejects unsupported changed income or gains. Spanish USD bank cash requires an explicit account-to-existing-fiscal-asset link. Every native December closing cash value updates that asset inside the same reciprocal assessment passes, preserving its supplied ownership and dated EUR quote. Other fiscal assets/debts remain independently supplied conditional inputs; market marks are not automatically statutory fiscal values. Same-year IP payments that exceed a cash-dependent revised bill remain unsupported by the native overpayment boundary; no refund entitlement or artificial payment credit is inferred. Payment authorization caps also remain fixed: a higher computed bill without sufficient scheduled authority fails funding rather than enlarging the payment.

Results retain the native country assessments and reconciliation alongside every path. Candidate diagnostics separate domestic and foreign net tax cash and show their sum. Original optimizer input/audit exports retain the fixed country context; selected country-input export retains both the scenario and Spain facts.
