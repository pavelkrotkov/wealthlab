# ADR 0063: QOZ reinvestment after the source year closes

Status: bounded next-year source-return consumer for [issue #12](https://github.com/pavelkrotkov/wealthlab/issues/12), above [ADR0062's actual QOF stock contract](0062-qoz-source-gain-contracts.md).

## Observation and return ownership

A direct stock sale can close its tax year before an actual qualifying investment. The original gain cannot be optimistically deferred using future cash. With `prior_source_year_revision_assumed_confirmed`, an actual next-year call may revise the actually closed source-year return after unused eligible gain and the 180-day clock have been checked. The bounded observation profile is the immediately following January–June; monthly cash conventions still require a month-end call. Expired gains remain ordinary-cost investments without the QOZ benefit. Unobserved source disposals, pass-through clocks and mandatory legacy deemed inclusions are not interchangeable with direct-sale gains.

The existing closed-return machinery keeps its public January wash-revision guard. A factored internal operation is called only after the QOZ consumer validates the supported original/current years and actually closed return. Each actual election adjusts the source-year capital character, not current-year income. Corrected capital carry and state-refund recovery reach the current annual case. Original payments, earlier refund receipts and historical cash rows remain intact. Only unpaid tax or the remaining refund queue changes; a newly known refund is scheduled strictly after observation. Repeated calls use the already amended source return and actual net-paid position, preventing duplicate refund payments.

Calls never borrow against a predicted refund. Prior tax arrears are paid before the call, and tax already paid reduces the cash available for investment. Actual funded cash caps the election. No source-year correction or instant cash appears when no eligible investment occurred. Original returns and successive corrected results remain together in the dated audit rather than rewriting a historical row as if a different payment had happened.

## Existing consumers and comparison

The existing IRA reconciliation rechecks completed MAGI while preserving posted deductions and nondeductible basis; it does not silently claim a newly possible deduction. Unsupported MAGI combinations still fail. The existing Medicare consumer updates future lookbacks, rejecting already billed redeterminations that need another billing adapter. The existing education consumer updates potential NJBEST deduction and records the QOZ finalization reason without rewriting funded elections.

Products offers a **2026 gain / 2027 investment** starter and displays original/revised source-year liability, remaining refund and actual receipt date beside household cash. The five policy runs are federal denied, deferral-only, conditional ten-year election, a **timely exit** using the same asset returns/leverage/fee/carry terms with only exit/settlement constraints removed, and the separately disclosed unlevered liquid reference. Delayed exit and benefit denial can therefore be examined without changing the underlying fund return assumptions. Every call has its own investment clock; source-stock holding time never becomes QOF holding time.

The month-end ordering remains observed returns on existing tranches, actual calls from available cash, ordinary dividends, ex-dividend inclusion/sale measurements, and due settlement. A new call earns no earlier return. Internal market/dealing locks govern modeled distributions/sales; subscription acceptance itself remains a supplied contract assumption. Underlying entity tax remains explicitly embedded in asset returns. These conditional C-corporation stock cases do not establish partnership/pass-through qualification, K-1 allocations, debt basis or sponsor terms.

## Authority and checks

The [statutory amendment effective-date rule](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t26/s1400Z-2/) selects the regime by investment after December 31, 2026. [Notice 2026-40 §4.02](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/n-26-40.pdf) describes anticipated regulations permitting timely post-2026 investment of pre-2027 gains; §4.01 distinguishes mandatory legacy deemed inclusion from a fresh eligible gain. These anticipated transition rules are not labeled final regulation. [NJ's official QOZ statement](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/taxation/federaltaxcuts.shtml) remains the starting point for the independently confirmed state profile, with no unsupported NJ-only federal election. Authorities were checked October 25, 2026; future annual tax rates remain supplied assumptions.

Actual household checks retain the original $100,000 stock plus $5,000 cash across a December sale, later call and delayed refund. They verify new-regime inclusion in 2032, original-year amendment, no negative current-year source gain, actual prepayment funding caps, a second election after the first refund, no reset of used gain after legacy inclusion, fixed IRA basis/deductions, corrected future Medicare tiers with historical bills retained, matched delayed/timely exits and real Streamlit transition submission. A mixed SSA-work case also keeps a $3,372 government claim unavailable: only a $562 actual receipt can fund a $1,000 optional subscription, leaving $438 unfilled and an independent $800 bill unfunded. Existing January return/recovery and IRA/Medicare/education regressions remain required.
