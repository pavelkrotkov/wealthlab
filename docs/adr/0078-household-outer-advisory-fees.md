# ADR 0078: Sourced outer advisory fees in actual household cash

Status: bounded arrears fee vertical for [issue #18](https://github.com/pavelkrotkov/wealthlab/issues/18). Product manager/broker charges and embedded policy costs remain separate.

## Sourced contracts and observed bases

`Scenario.advisory_fee_plan` contains independently sourced schedules with known source/availability dates, explicit covered accounts and bases, dated periods, actual-day denominators of 360/365/366, progressive marginal AUM tiers and a fixed charge per period. Marginal tiers price only their own band; no top rate is applied to all AUM. The variable charge rounds once before the sourced fixed charge is added. Sources and terms must be resolved and known before accrual begins. The original editable defaults are invented examples, not a quoted adviser contract.

Accrual start/end (end exclusive), valuation date, assessment date and contractual payment due date are separate fields. This first profile supports observed month-end valuation/assessment/due dates and arrears; a quarterly arrears period retains its entire supplied accrual span. Advance billing, unavailable historical snapshots or different valuation/payment stages fail explicitly. They never shorten the accrual period or substitute a forecast supplied base. Actual cash payment dates remain distinct and can lag a due date when cash is insufficient.

`modeled_net_account_equity` uses the actual supported account balance after native product marks/receipts. Native option collateral, financing and noncash contract value remain inside that book's net equity; its released cash is in the household cash account. Exchange-manager charges already reduce native NAV. Employer unvested/contingent rights are excluded. Insurance requires explicit `life_net_cash_value` or `life_net_surrender_value` from current native policy observations, net of actual policy loan debt. Gross/borrowed/unvested assets, death protection and unsupported property or early-stage HSA/529 closing bases are not reconstructed as advisory AUM. Active invested-HSA/529 marks execute in later hooks, so their closing bases are unavailable at this named earlier stage.

Each required covered account must exist. A source-confirmed `if_present` entry permits an optional investment opportunity to be absent and records that absence explicitly. It does not silently forgive an unknown required account. Zero absent AUM does not waive a separately supplied fixed contractual charge. Every schedule uses the same observed snapshot on its date, before any fee payment, so cash-inclusive charges do not depend on schedule order.

## Timing, cash and required funding

An assessment book follows native option/product marks and receipts, immediately before optional `SavingLedger` purchases. Saving already reads the dynamic required-cash quote, now including the observed bill. Later optional HSA/529/IRA hooks temporarily reserve the assessed total fee obligation on the existing wallet. Reservation moves no funds, creates no extra account or liability and ends with `try/finally`; deposits can use only unreserved cash. Current payroll priorities remain unchanged.

When a required native wrapper payment calls `required_funding.fund`, that operation temporarily suspends the reservation. The existing solver compares actual gross cash to the unchanged full required quote, including the fee, then restores the reservation before optional hooks resume. This avoids counting the fee twice and avoids clipping a reservation larger than existing cash. With $0 gross cash, a $100 fee and a $100 required payment, native funding must raise $200, pay $100 and retain $100 for the fee. A later actual $150 receipt reduces the same sale to $50. Locked or partial sources cannot create borrowing or spend an unfunded reserve.

The ordinary `flows.fees` payment receives the new assessment as an additional current bill. Actual payments use the existing wallet's complete ordered cash pool, validated exactly against the plan's IDs. Narrower payment scopes are unsupported. Generic and advisory arrears remain in the existing `fees_owed` liability; net wealth subtracts it once and normal funding-failure checks retain unpaid bills. Payment attribution is explicit: prior generic, prior advisory, current generic, current advisory. The audit separates advisory assessment, actual paid cash and remaining liability. Outer fees do not alter product basis, create a tax event or receive an automatic tax deduction.

## UI, checks and remaining acceptance

Household edits sourced schedules/coverage/tiers in JSON; Markets offers original hypothetical outer fees on the same realized paths and retains editable assumptions. Both show actual assessment/payment/liability and the complete dated audit. Nested audit records remain outside numeric plotting frames and source changes reset advisory form drafts.

Independent original examples verify $100,000 AUM at 1%, 30 actual days/360 produces $83.33; a prior 10% observed price shock produces $91.67. A $150,000 marginal schedule with 1% on the first $100,000, 0.5% on the remainder and a $7 fixed charge produces $111.17. Two cash-inclusive schedules both observe the same $1,000 before payment. Optional saving buys $990 from $1,000 while retaining a $10 fee; optional HSA/529 deposits retain a $100 fee. Actual medical funding raises only the remaining bill plus fee and native commission. Native option, exchange, employer and life checks preserve actual tax/basis/embedded-cost books.

Before final adviser-fee fidelity, a dependent dated-snapshot profile must support source-confirmed advance billing and opening valuation where actual contract terms require them. It needs stored actually observed opening snapshots, full independent accrual dates, explicit due/payment timing and no future marks. Root owns reading actual private adviser terms and optimizer choice/comparability integration; this bounded vertical does not claim that all adviser contracts or final fee acceptance are complete.
