# Opening sleeve allocations from existing liquid cash

The optimizer can compare fixed new-investment opportunities as well as policies for existing accounts. Each opportunity supplies one complete ordinary long/short or limited-liability private-fund unit, its owner, an existing USD cash source, integer unit bounds, and explicit confirmations for a hypothetical opening cash purchase, proportional dollar schedules and shared market paths. A policy choice selects units by opportunity ID; separate dimensions can control and lock separate opportunities. Duplicate ownership of the same decision is rejected.

## Capital and assumptions

Candidates begin immediately after the hypothetical purchases. Compilation debits the designated original cash account and adds only the purchased sleeve capital. Existing noncash accounts, lots, tax bases, household background, fees and spending remain unchanged. Combined purchases cannot spend future wages, tax deductions or cash in an unselected source account. Each candidate reports original/allocated opening wealth, actual source cash debits and new account capital. The native sleeve books charge their entry costs and execute subsequent returns, collateral, redemptions, settlement and taxes once. This is a projection assumption, not an executed trade or a new deposit.

All offered contracts validate even when their choice is locked out or set to zero. Destinations must be new, distinct accounts; a current holding cannot be relabelled as a fresh investment. Unit count scales only opening equity, supplied monetary tax allocations, explicit redemption amounts, and private-fund outside bases, basis adjustments and request amounts. Dates, rates, market factors, gates, settlement terms, tax character, loss eligibility and release dates stay fixed. Household tax limits still apply to the resulting totals. Scaling a supplied allocation is an explicit assumption, not a tax loss generated from the economic path. Original sleeve market observations require their own confirmation; activating a new opportunity cannot confirm them implicitly.

For this bounded new cash-only private-fund profile, independently supplied initial federal and NJ unit bases must both equal the cash purchase cost. Differing historical basis, inherited/gifted interests, in-kind contributions and changes in investor debt shares require a different adapter. Subsequent federal/NJ basis adjustments remain independent. Federal context was checked against [IRS Publication541, revised December2025, page13, Basis of Partner’s Interest](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p541.pdf#page=13), using the installed public tax-reference collection and current official page on October25,2026. The publication explains contributed money/property basis and the separate effects of liabilities. The equal-NJ-cost entry is an explicitly supplied profile boundary, not a claim that all federal and NJ bases are interchangeable.

## UI and independent checks

The synthetic cash-allocation menu composes with the existing retirement, account, diversification, fixed-product and robustness starters. It compares retained cash, two ordinary margin units, two private-fund units and one of each. Each unit costs $10,000; purchases preserve the original household’s $300,000 total capital. The default safety and concentration targets remain enabled, and unknown issuer holdings are still unknown. The user may edit all finite choices, bounds and locks.

Flat, zero-cost cases all end at $251,999 after $48,000 spending and the existing $1 brokerage exit commission. With a 130/30 profile, $20,000 capital creates $32,000 gross positions. A 0.1% entry cost and 0.1% exit cost, with no intermediate rebalancing or other costs, reduce each invested mix by $64 to $251,935; keeping cash retains $251,999. The native fund recovers its actual paid basis before calculating exit recognition; a loss carry is not counted as cash. An insufficient-cash mix is visibly unsupported rather than silently borrowed or partially funded.

This implements new cash allocations for ordinary sleeves and the supported conditional private-fund profile. Exchange contributions need actual source-lot transfers, life alternatives need complete matched illustrations, and other product/options/cross-border allocation adapters remain required for issue18. No interpolation of insurer values or rewrite of already-owned assets is introduced.
