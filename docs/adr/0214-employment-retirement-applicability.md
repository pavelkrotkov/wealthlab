# Source-matched retirement and employer compensation

Status: conditional native-profile integration, October26,2026. Extends the existing [employment book](0014-employer-compensation.md) and [native employer exits](0201-native-option-employer-search.md). No new cash, tax, vesting-law or basis engine is introduced.

## Applicability is a source fact

Changing a member's retirement date stops retirement-linked wages; that alone establishes no employer forfeiture, continued vesting, payout right or tax treatment. A fixed former-employer award may legitimately be independent of a later job. Common account ownership establishes neither relationship. Optimizer cases containing a compensation plan now require one of two explicit source profiles:

- `employer_independence`: a resolved, dated source covering every original tranche exactly once, with confirmation that the complete contract is independent of every candidate employment/retirement choice and remains valid across the entered income and market envelope. Existing synthetic former-employer contract menus provide an explicitly hypothetical independence record.
- `employment_offers`: complete native `EmploymentPlan`/`CompensationPlan` profiles, each tied to an exact all-member retirement-date map and available at the original decision. Every retirement choice atomically selects its matching `employment_offer`; the source map and actual compiled member dates must agree. One date map has one unambiguous source profile, preventing the search from choosing competing contractual assumptions for the same retirement decision. The original baseline must itself match a supplied profile so robustness comparisons retain a sourced starting case.

All offered profiles and inactive/locked choices are validated. Original accounts, raw income streams, tax context, lot bases, vested/contingent tranches, cash rights, liquidity permissions and previously compiled allocation facts remain fixed. Within complete supplied profiles, dated employment changes and compensation events can differ. Wage-path identities, owners, covered streams and opening multipliers remain shared. Native work-state, date/sequence, owner/tax, source-allocation and account validation are reused before execution. Changes to already compiled allocations require returning to the original unprepared source scenario rather than silently dropping posted taxes.

The selected native profile executes through the existing shared market adapter, wage splitting, compensation preparation, tax ledger and household wallet. Vesting does not deposit money; actual distributions debit the remaining vested book before their single cash receipt. Source tax allocations remain independent of received cash. Source confirmations describe conditional input validity; they are not legal verification. A missing supplied profile, unknown source or mismatched date cannot silently retain the later scenario's vesting.

## Visible example and independent accounting

Optimize offers **Source-matched retirement and employer vesting**. The original synthetic household has $300,000 cash/stock capital plus $20,000 vested employer value and a separate $80,000 contingent award. Both candidates retain the same $320,000 vested opening wealth; the contingent amount is not opening spendable capital. The source explicitly forfeits that award on early retirement or vests it after one additional working year. The example preserves default age100/95%/two-year-reserve targets; its two-year horizon does not establish retirement safety.

The flat-price acceptance case uses zero trading commission to isolate the employment decision and actual settled cash. Early retirement earns no wages, receives its existing $20,000 vested value once, forfeits $80,000 and spends $48,000: **$272,000 final cash** with zero modeled tax. Later retirement earns $60,000 W-2 wages and vests the contingent $80,000 before receiving $100,000 employer cash. Frozen2025 rules produce $8,535 tax in2026 and $10,384 in2027, leaving $320,000+$60,000+$80,000−$48,000−$18,919=**$393,081 final cash**. Training and held-out execution agree; native audits show actual payout, forfeiture, closed rights, no remaining employer position and no unpaid tax or refund receivable.

The ordinary federal calculations are $2,943 income tax plus $4,590 employee payroll on the wage year, and $7,743 on the independently supplied $100,000 employer allocation in the following year. NJ contributes $1,002 and $2,641 respectively under the entered independent classification. Existing [dated primary tax references and limitations](../tax-rules.md) remain authoritative; no new legal classification is inferred here.

A market lock at the later redemption leaves an actual employer holding, so its higher marked wealth cannot outrank completed early cash. Fixed dollar requests and independently supplied tax facts remain conditional under other market paths; insufficient redemptions, residual balances and unproven contractual closure withhold completed-exit ranking. Changing source legal terms is a separate scenario assumption, not an optimization control.

## Input and release boundary

Private scenarios continue through the existing editable JSON/import workflow. They are never regenerated or reconfirmed by a synthetic factory. Users supply complete applicable profiles for their own permitted dates; the tool does not invent a retirement eligibility rule, current-employer link, future illustration or forfeiture. Raw standalone Household scenarios still execute their explicitly supplied native events; automatic profile selection belongs to the bounded policy-search input above. Current integrated checks and independent review remain required before release.
