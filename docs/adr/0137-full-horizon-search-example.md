# A complete age-100 search example

The short composable examples deliberately fail the default retirement horizon. The Optimize workspace now also offers a separate full-horizon cash/stock example with native controls for starting cash, monthly W-2 wages, essential monthly spending and the younger member's target age. These controls create a new synthetic case; they do not extend private files or invent missing personal, employer, insurer or fund facts.

The endpoint reuses the same target-date calculation as the funding report: the younger member must finish the entire target-age year, through the next birthday. A later endpoint completes the calendar tax year when necessary. January 1 birthdays, non-January birthdays and leap-day births are checked. The fixed synthetic members born in 1975 and 1977 require January 1,2078 for the default age 100 target, giving 624 monthly periods. A previous exploratory 50-year case correctly failed this horizon requirement despite reconciling its cash.

Every native lot mark, annual tax case, income/expense end date, market sample and final stock-sale date covers the generated horizon. Price placeholders are explicitly synthetic and all are replaced by the common realized market path. Actual dividends, permitted funding sales, separate tax bases, costs, returns and terminal liquidation use the existing household engine. Wages stop at the selected retirement date; essential spending follows the shared inflation path. The finite retirement choices, locks, market assumptions and sample sizes remain editable in the existing JSON editor.

The tax template explicitly freezes 2025 federal/NJ assumptions for each future year; it is not a prediction of future law. Native rule and age boundaries remain enforced and an unsupported target is displayed as an input error. Cash earns no modeled interest. The default training sample has 4 paths and the independent validation sample has 8; these are small execution demonstrations and do not establish a reliable 95% retirement-success probability. The 95% target, two-year reserve and issuer-coverage constraint remain enabled. Historical/named-stress comparisons and complete product schedules can be entered through the existing schema, but the example does not fabricate them for a long period.

The independent zero-wage/zero-return/full-basis case starts with $2,100,000 and spends $2,000 for 624 months. Its actual terminal cash is $851,999 after the $1 stock-sale commission. Both training and held-out execution must agree, all 52 tax years must resolve and the age/reserve/exit/issuer gates must pass. The small-sample confidence interval remains visible even in this mechanically certain flat illustration.

This example makes a complete horizon runnable and editable. It does not complete the remaining product-allocation, cross-border or release acceptance work, and it is not a personalized retirement conclusion.

Validation: 23 financial/UI/compatibility checks pass with 98.16% combined statement/branch coverage of affected modules; both new modules measure 100%. The default stochastic benchmark exceeded 310 seconds and was interrupted for profiling. Repeated identical federal calculations took 0.897 seconds for 20 calls; performance remains follow-up work before the long-horizon workflow is considered ready.
