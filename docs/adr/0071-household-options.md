# ADR 0071: Actual household option cash, assets and recognition

Status: dependent household layer for issue #10. It uses ADR0070's option contracts and tested lot engine; generated-market bindings remain explicitly unsupported.

`option_plan` owns one complete taxable USD account. Its opening stock and inside cash must reconcile exactly to the existing household account; a separate standalone reserve must be zero. The account cannot overlap a lot book, long-short sleeve, generic contribution destination or generic proportional-basis tax funding source. Every month requires a supplied final-day accounting mark through the exact household horizon. No option price, volatility path, daily quote history or exercise decision is manufactured from household market factors.

After actual household receipts and prior tax arrears, the persistent option book receives access to the finite current Wallet cash. It executes dated premiums, supplied American assignments, expiry elections, exercise purchases/sales, dividend payments and separately priced rolls. Household incomes use the existing monthly convention; this does not certify daily settlement liquidity. Broker collateral uses the same explicit conditional formula as the standalone workbench. Only actual unreserved inside cash returns to Wallet before other sleeves and optional wrapper deposits. Noncash model marks and reserved collateral cannot pay household bills. Loans reduce option equity, accrue their configured costs and remain distinct from household income; negative account equity is a separate liability deducted exactly once.

Physical share deliveries use the existing `Book` for actual shares and separate federal/NJ bases. Actual share disposals and supported option recognition become zero-cash `TaxEvent`s in the existing annual federal/NJ engine. The premium and strike cash already moved through Wallet/book; recognition cannot deposit it again. Existing withholding, annual settlement, tax shortfalls and spending checks observe the resulting household cash. All legal/holding-period and independent federal/NJ option assumptions must be declared; an unresolved rule blocks the household result, rather than silently creating zero tax or a tax saving. Optional ordinary dividends require an additional explicit cash-receipt tax assumption; qualified dividends and special January recognition are unfinished.

The new Household form loads an invented annual example: 100 shares, $10,000 opening stock value, $1,000 household cash, one written 110 call with $500 premium, $500 monthly essential spending and a supplied February expiry at stock120. Actual assignment sells the shares for $11,000; the shared annual engine sees $6,500 federal/$5,500 NJ gain because the original bases differ. The low-income synthetic case owes zero under its declared rules and ends with $6,500 cash after $6,000 spending. An independent higher-income case verifies actual year-end tax cash and no duplicated recognition. Another case removes stock-call collateral coverage: actual cash becomes reserved and spending fails. Physical call exercise creates 100 additional shares and separate updated bases; ordinary dividends add cash and annual income once.

Funding failure includes unresolved option execution and actual collateral/spending shortfalls. A failed expired physical delivery stays open as an obligation, even if a hypothetical unwind sells other stock. The book does not invent a retroactive option close. Future optimization can consume these actual household projections; market-path option pricing needs a complete stock/index/volatility/rate/dividend binding and remains a separate guarded adapter.

Primary tax and valuation authorities and unresolved legal cases are recorded in [ADR0070](0070-dated-options.md). Checks cover actual assignment/exercise cash, shared annual liabilities, finite collateral, independent bases, ordinary dividend receipts, missing monthly marks, conflicting books, legal-assumption guards and the real Household form.
