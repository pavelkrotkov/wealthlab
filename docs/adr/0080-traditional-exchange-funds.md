# ADR 0080: Conditional traditional exchange funds and actual household liquidity

Status: implemented vertical slice of #11. All examples are original synthetic inputs. No sponsor, adviser, actual household inventory or legal eligibility is represented.

Products / **Traditional exchange funds** edits the complete household and contract JSON and compares the exchange policy with an immediate direct sale. Entered marks or the existing deterministic, stress or seeded Monte Carlo generator supply a single selected realized path to both policies. The source stock and every basket security have explicit price-only marks. Cash yields in a generated batch block this adapter; cash distributions and tax allocations require their own contract inputs. A generated lock in any supplied factor blocks that month's product requests. Submitted market inputs, seed/path choice, realized prices and all household rows are exported.

## Capital, basis and economics

One USD taxable account owns one actual source lot. Its opening value must reconcile to the actual shares and opening price. A specified partial contribution uses the existing exact lot split; retained shares preserve independent federal/NJ basis. Rejected acceptance leaves the original shares intact and charges no fund fees. Account overlap with other lot/product books, generic contributions or generic taxable funding is rejected. Ordinary stock/ETF securities with distinct wash groups are supported; other security classifications block this adapter.

Accepted shares leave the stock book and become partnership NAV, with independent outside federal/NJ basis. Conditional deferred contribution carries basis; denied nonrecognition realizes the corresponding basis gain without creating cash and increases that jurisdiction's basis. Taxable loss contributions need a separate legal/wash adapter and block this profile. Direct sales use the actual lot trade/wash ledger, realize the source gain/loss and reinvest gross proceeds in the same initial diversified basket. Direct purchases acquire actual purchase-cost basis rather than the partnership's supplied basis weights.

The fund follows a supplied buy-and-hold basket benchmark before fees; it is not a reconstruction of sponsor holdings or returns. Annual fees / 12 are charged against each month-end marked NAV, capped at NAV. Direct ownership has an explicit zero-fee assumption in this profile. Fund distributions reduce NAV and deposit household cash once. Separately supplied tax allocations create zero-cash tax events. Signed basis adjustments and cash basis reductions keep both jurisdictions independent; neither manager fees nor recognized income automatically invent a deduction or basis change. Negative basis, distributions above NAV or adjusted basis, and unusable share precision block calculation. Excess-basis distributions, debt, partial redemptions and tax rules for actual sponsor allocations require another adapter.

## Release, early redemption and eventual sale

Contractual availability and dated request order are explicit; reaching a date does not automatically distribute or sell assets. A pre-release request either remains denied or performs a specifically permitted **full cash exit**, with the supplied early fee and request-specific confirmed tax recognition. Missing early tax confirmation blocks the latter. The default contract denies early redemption.

A permitted post-lock request distributes a basket with explicit market-value weights, separate supplied federal/NJ basis weights and federal holding starts. Every allocation sums to one independently. These are conditional legal inputs, not a statutory basis-allocation algorithm; partnership asset bases and other missing facts must be supplied and verified outside this adapter. Denied basket nonrecognition recognizes that jurisdiction's positive appreciation and adds it to basis before the actual basket is created. Cash rounding reduces outside basis equally before the remaining basis is allocated. Ordinary `Lot` and `Book` objects then own the received shares.

A dated `sell_liquid` request sells the full diversified basket through the existing actual-share trade ledger. The supplied federal holding starts determine gain character; direct ownership uses its actual acquisition date. Retained source stock is never silently sold by that basket request. Federal wash handling uses existing matching and explicitly confirmed absence of external substantially identical trades for the forward window. NJ separately selects its conditional wash policy; unconfirmed NJ treatment blocks execution. A taxable basket sale realizes the remaining embedded gain: deferral is not permanent elimination.

## Actual household consequences

The product runs at the existing after-receipts hook, after prior tax arrears and before optional wrapper deposits and current taxes/spending. The existing tax ledger recognizes contribution, allocation, early-exit and actual-sale events in the actual annual household return. Existing cash funding, federal/NJ payments, deductions, refund timing, carry and unpaid tax positions remain authoritative. No hypothetical tax-saving receipt is deposited.

Direct gross reinvestment leaves its contribution tax payable from household cash or explicit dated basket sales. This profile never automatically sells a product to pay a bill. A liquid basket, locked NAV and spendable wallet cash are reported separately. Denied redemption can therefore cause actual consumption/tax shortfalls despite positive investment wealth. Unredeemed fund NAV remains in the restricted-value column until a request actually distributes it. Ending net wealth with unsold holdings includes deferred embedded gains, so it must not be read as estimated spendable terminal wealth. Final sale cases show actual resulting taxes and cash; the synthetic eight-year horizon is not a retirement-success claim.

## Authority and checks

Checked October 24, 2026 against the current [IRS Publication 541 (12/2025)](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p541), following local tax-reference discovery and reading its supporting source passages. Printed pages 6–10 describe distribution recognition, marketable securities, the seven-year precontribution-gain rule, investment-company contributions and distributed-property basis. These rules have exceptions and require facts beyond elapsed time. Seven years alone does not establish a tax-free exchange-fund contribution or basket distribution. NJ treatment remains independently conditional. Actual sponsor acceptance, partnership qualification, contributed-property dispositions, liabilities, inside asset bases, section 704(c), eligible-partner status, fees and redemption terms remain unverified user inputs.

Independent checks conserve a flat $130,000 household through eventual gain recognition and actual tax payment; retain exact bases after partial contribution; reconcile a 12% annual monthly fee convention to $11,361.52 of twelve-month NAV charges; distinguish a $1,000 distribution from separate taxable income/basis adjustments; and compare a denied locked withdrawal's $10,000 spending shortfall with an actual liquid-basket sale. Early cash fees/recognition, rejected acceptance, denied jurisdiction treatments, shared immutable market paths, scope/date/basis guards and actual editable Streamlit submission are tested.
