# Uninsured households and a zero-opening supplied new-issue path

An explicit `LifeHouseholdPlan` may contain no holdings while retaining
`insurance_need_by_owner`. The existing household policy adapter then reports
zero policy cash, assets, basis, loans, premiums, reserves and death protection,
and the entire independently supplied need remains a protection shortfall.
Removing a policy does not remove the need or replace death protection with wealth.
Every insurance account still requires exactly one actual holding.

The insurance comparison may name no term accounts for an uninsured reference.
There is no term-price confirmation to invent when no protection is purchased.
The existing independently priced term checks continue whenever term accounts
are supplied. Every other reference holding still requires an actual supplied
opening surrender or lapse; existing financial policy history and capital must
remain identical across alternatives. This does not turn an existing contract
into a zero-cost new policy or allow it to disappear without settlement.

## Actual new-issue cash path

Products / **Whole life versus liquid saving** adds the separate **No policy
versus new issue** starter. Both alternatives open with exactly $10,000 household
cash, no investment shares, the same $1,000 monthly spending, independent $150,000
insurance need and identical household/tax/market background. The original
existing-policy and nonzero-opening-coverage examples retain their distinct inputs.

The new example has zero opening cash/surrender/death values, federal investment,
loan debt and insurance account equity. It supplies dated $1,000 January and
February premiums, independent post-payment values and $100,000 protection, and
an actual March $2,100 full surrender. These are original hypothetical conditional
inputs, **not an insurer illustration, price, guarantee, underwriting result or
qualification determination**. No values or coverage are scaled or generated from
premium totals. A different payment path requires its own supplied values and
settlements; changing a stop date alone does not establish paid-up status.

The existing live wallet pays the actual premiums, the existing policy book adds
only paid premiums to investment, and the supplied terminal receipt closes the
policy. Each required payment has an independently supplied applicable failure
settlement: zero after the unfunded first premium, or $900 after the unfunded
second premium. A failure closes the policy and cancels later events and coverage.
An unfunded premium creates no premium basis or free protection. A separate
supplied delayed-premium test has zero protection before its actual first payment.
The engine retains its existing monthly household execution convention; this is
not a daily insurer-coverage or grace-period engine.

## Tax boundary and verification

independently, not copied from federal basis. Local tax-reference discovery did
not return a supporting current life-surrender passage; related 2024 or state
results were not treated as 2025 federal authority. The actual federal passage
was read in [IRS Publication525 (2025), “Surrender of policy for cash”](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p525),
which identifies proceeds above investment as income. The existing section72
scope, NIIT adapter and unsupported-contract boundaries remain unchanged.

Independent checks reconcile the uninsured $7,000 ending cash against the funded
$7,100 ending cash, $2,000 actually paid investment and $100 federal/supplied NJ
surrender income. Death protection is separate throughout and returns to zero
on the executed exit. A higher independently supplied identical annual-income
baseline produces $28.37 additional actual tax, reducing the $100 gross advantage
to $71.63 after recognized tax and cash payment. With only $500 available and no
spending, the first premium fails and all $500 remains without coverage; with
$1,500 available, one premium is paid, the next fails, and the actual $900
settlement leaves $1,400. Later illustrated receipts cannot reappear.
