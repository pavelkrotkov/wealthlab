## Decision

Reuse native `property` accounts, the existing USD cash wallet, actual mutable household debt balances and the annual federal/NJ tax ledger. Add a complete sourced personal-home profile with fixed independent opening bases, one observed value and sale-lock flag per modeled month, explicit retention or one declared month-end sale, gross proceeds/costs, an authorization cap for existing owner cash, and separately supplied prelimited federal/NJ taxable gains. Source provenance and availability are checked; the original input scenario remains unchanged.

Property marks change actual native account values. A retained home contributes marked equity after its existing liens, never liquid spending cash. No market factor return, quote, home-price index, automatic tax exclusion or mortgage-interest deduction is inferred. The supplied observations can describe alternative home-value assumptions; shared market runs retain those observations and state this limit beside their loader.

The property book executes after required native products and before common advisory assessment and optional saving. A close first quotes all linked native principal and full current-month contractual interest, plus supplied closing costs. Gross escrow proceeds satisfy these obligations before a residual reaches the household cash pool. A shortfall can consume only existing cash within the explicit authorization cap. An unfunded or locked close mutates no closing cash, principal, property disposition or taxable gain; the ordinary observed mark and normal debt service remain. The request is not automatically retried, and its failure remains visible in household funding and market joint feasibility.

A completed close debits any owner contribution once, sets the original property account and actual linked debt balances to zero, and deposits only net residual cash once. Its tax event has zero cash received: separately supplied recognition enters the original annual ledger without redepositing proceeds. Generic debt service then sees the paid-off native balances. The shared required-cash quote now reads live debt balances when invoked, so optional saving cannot reserve a fictitious mortgage installment after payoff. Fees observe actual supported account values; uncovered home value does not silently become advisory AUM.

## Scope

This first property adapter supports complete calendar months, USD personal-use homes, unchanged supplied basis and explicit liens already present in the household debt table. It rejects rental/business use, acquisition/improvement cash flows, missing/late/unresolved marks, unconfirmed closing withholding or escrow conditions, duplicate lien linkage, unsupported taxes or inferred exemptions, and positive separately entered mortgage-interest deductions when linked debt lacks dated deduction attribution. These are explicit limits, not a general property-tax or title/closing engine. Supplied taxable gains cannot exceed independently calculated net economic gains; legal eligibility must be established outside the numerical adapter. There is no automatic new loan, debt forgiveness, sale retry, brokerage liquidation for owner cash, or property optimizer choice in this layer. The complete optimizer and cross-border consumers remain separate integration work.
