# ADR 0070: Dated option contracts and complete economic accounting

Status: core option workbench for issue #10; household adapter follows in a dependent layer.

The Products / Options overlays workspace edits independently invented covered-call, collar and four-leg stock/index scenarios. Each contract states strikes, opening/expiry dates, contract count, multiplier, premium, fees, European/American style, cash/physical delivery and expiry election. A roll closes the existing leg at a separately supplied execution quote and opens a new ID with its own price and terms. Partial closes require separate legs. Physical index delivery, short stock delivery, automatic exercise prediction and unsupported lot overlays reject or visibly fail.

QuantLib 1.44 prices European options analytically and American options with an 801-step CRR tree under supplied ACT/365 flat rates, continuous dividend yields and volatilities. Explicit cash dividends have ex-date entitlements and payment dates; continuous pricing yields do not create dividend cash. These approximations do not model discrete-dividend exercise decisions. Every opening, expiry, event and year-end requires an explicit dated spot mark. Model values and synthetic paths are labelled; no market quote history is fetched, fabricated or certified.

Premiums enter actual cash once. Cash settlement recognizes signed payoff minus premium and fees. Physical exercises use the existing lot `Book` for actual strike purchases or specific share disposals, with its FIFO selection and independent federal/NJ bases. Conditional ordinary option mechanics capitalize long-call cost/reduce written-put basis; long-put cost reduces share proceeds and written-call premium increases proceeds. Opening fees follow the premium; closing fees apply to close/exercise transactions, including the actual delivery trade. A separately supplied expiry fee applies to worthless/elected lapses. Those tax adjustments never create another cash payment. Insufficient owned shares or physical-purchase funding leave an unresolved delivery obligation visible. Complete unwind closes still-live options at hypothetical model marks and sells actual remaining shares; an expired failed delivery cannot be magically bought back. Unpaid cash settlements remain liabilities and can exceed initial capital.

A finite external reserve can fund actual deficits. Settlement borrowing is separately elected, retained as debt and charged an explicit simple ACT/365 annual rate. No interest deduction is inferred. Collateral is a supplied conservative broker scenario: uncovered short units reserve current option value plus configured fraction of spot; cash-secured puts substitute strike for the spot component. Stock-call coverage consumes actual owned shares across short calls once. It is segregated cash, never a second expense or borrowed income. Observed shortfalls remain explicit; this is not a certified broker or intraday margin engine. Stock/index stress independently revalues all opening-date legs and stock on a two-dimensional grid; one put does not imply a floor on the aggregate portfolio.

## Conditional tax boundary

Federal and NJ recognition remain separate from cash and from tax savings. Unresolved straddle, constructive-sale, holding-period, option/share wash-history or jurisdiction assumptions produce `recognized: null`, with named missing assumptions. Ordinary realization only runs under explicit declared capital treatment. The user must affirm unaffected supplied holding periods and independent NJ premium/basis mechanics. The engine does not decide qualified-covered-call status, protective-put holding-period changes, constructive-sale eligibility, mixed/identified-straddle elections, substantially-identical wash sales, dividend qualification or special written-call long-term loss character. Federal and NJ automatic wash tracking in the nested lot case are rejected; conditional recognition requires an explicit assumption that option/share wash-sale restrictions do not apply. Those cases require an adviser-supported assumption or a separate adapter; unresolved statuses cannot enter a household tax result as zero.

A separately classified cash-settled section1256 leg marks federal gains at supplied December31 accounting marks, resets subsequent recognition and allocates 60% long/40% short. The supplied mark must represent the last-business-day value; no holiday quote is invented. NJ continues to actual realization under an independent conditional profile. Supplied per-recognition federal/NJ deferrals cannot exceed the actual loss. Deferred amounts are explicitly retained rather than automatically released or converted into a refund. Complete household election/history and legal eligibility are outside this bounded book.
