# Whole-life and insurance-alternative coverage

This index maps issue14's requested behavior to executable supported profiles. It does not establish an actual insurer quote, contract qualification or user tax eligibility. The comparison layer must pass its current-head review and CI before this index can support closing the ticket.

| Accepted behavior | Implementation and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Separate premiums, cash/surrender value and death protection; timing-based returns | [Supplied illustration adapter](adr/0100-supplied-whole-life-illustrations.md), [cash/loan identities](../tests/test_life_illustration_cash.py) and [native illustration UI](../tests/test_life_illustration_ui.py). Existing QuantLib dated NPV/root solving is reused; a dividend interest rate is not a policy IRR. |
| Loan principal/interest, withdrawals, surrender/lapse, conditional MEC tax and configurable stop-funding | [Contract cash/tax adapter](adr/0102-whole-life-contract-cash-and-tax.md), [actual contract cash](../tests/test_life_contract_cash.py), [conditional taxes](../tests/test_life_contract_tax.py) and [executed household recognition](../tests/test_life_household_tax.py). Changed funding paths require consistent supplied values and failure settlements. |
| Unknown values remain unknown; no invented guarantees | [Illustration guards](../tests/test_life_illustration_guards.py), [unknown contract recognition](../tests/test_life_contract_unknowns.py) and [household completeness guards](../tests/test_life_household_validation.py). Unresolved values/tax profiles cannot establish household funding success. |
| Household reserve, retirement premiums and independently supplied insurance need | [Actual household funding](adr/0103-household-whole-life-funding.md), [dated reserve/retirement checks](../tests/test_life_household_cash.py) and [failed-payment behavior](../tests/test_life_household_failures.py). Actual policy debt reduces living surrender equity once; death protection stays separate. |
| Limited/longer premiums versus liquid saving and separately priced protection | [Matched comparison](adr/0104-insurance-and-liquid-saving-alternatives.md), [independent capital/cost checks](../tests/test_life_comparison.py), [existing policy/loan surrender checks](../tests/test_life_comparison_existing.py), [all market methods](../tests/test_life_comparison_markets.py), [actual saving/wash mechanics](../tests/test_saving_tax.py) and [editable native UI](../tests/test_life_comparison_ui.py). |

Guaranteed/current/stressed values are separately supplied schedules. The adapters do not manufacture insurer assumptions from market returns or silently continue the original illustration after funding changes. Term prices and coverage are independent inputs, and its failed-payment exit cannot produce investment cash. Existing policy history must remain matched when comparing retention with surrender.

The annual federal profile uses explicit personal-contract assumptions; NJ recognition is independently supplied. Unsupported aggregation, special basis adjustments, seven-pay testing, automatic lapse/loan rights and below-debt discharge require additional validated profiles. Marked comparison wealth deducts recognized liabilities, not unexecuted future exit taxes. Users can supply actual terminal policy events and existing dated sell-all policies to execute exits. No protection, liquidity or retirement-safety equivalence is inferred from a wealth difference.
