# Model coverage and release limits

October 27, 2026. This matrix describes the implemented local workbench. [Release verification](release-validation.md) records executed checks; [issue #19](https://github.com/pavelkrotkov/wealthlab/issues/19) tracks final acceptance. Each workbench retains its explicit assumptions; this table does not replace its owning ADR.

| Area | Current UI / verification path | Material boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Household | Household editor, baseline clone, selected private save/import and shared CLI reports | Enabled plans only; the default synthetic example omits taxes. Monthly sequencing does not prove intramonth liquidity. |
| Federal/NJ | Annual tax; Household tax assumptions and annual settlement/audit | Published versus frozen future rules, independent state basis/categories and supplied recognition. [Tax coverage](tax-rules.md) remains authoritative for unsupported forms. |
| Gold / collectibles | Optimize / Gold treated as collectible sale timing; native lot input/import | Explicit synthetic collectible classification, zero cash yield, separate dated bases and actual sale taxes. No inferred gold-fund classification, issuer distribution or stock-style replacement harvesting. |
| Lots and concentration | Tax lots; actual-lot Household loaders; Markets staged/concentration/harvest/rebalance cases | Actual dated lots, permitted sales, wash relationships and lock coverage are required; no invented quote or absent basis. |
| Retirement | Retirement workbenches and named Household loaders | [Retirement coverage](retirement-coverage.md) lists supported claims, wrappers and exceptions. The recommended full install includes Owl production FRA/RMD data; core-only installs block applicable cases. Whole-record tax, benefit or RMD completeness is not inferred from a balance. |
| Property and debt | Household / synthetic sourced home and lien close; Markets / Sourced home and lien close; Optimize / fixed-home checkbox | Native USD personal-home marks, retained equity and dated actual principal/full-month-interest payoff share one cash/tax ledger. Complete supplied tax facts and owner cash authorization are required; blocked closes fail joint safety. Fixed property context participates in policy search; actual closure is required for completed-exit ranking. Rental/foreign rules, changing basis, inferred exclusions/deductible interest and automatic property choices are unsupported. |
| Employment | Household employment/employer-compensation/linked-shock loaders; Optimize source-matched retirement/vesting starter | Supplied vesting, withholding, employment/asset correlation and contractual rights. Optimizer contracts require sourced independence or complete native profiles for exact retirement dates; no owner-based employer linkage or automatic legal forfeiture. |
| Markets | Markets factors and Household funding with matched realized paths | Finite supplied horizons, sample uncertainty, explicit historical coverage and no assumed future quotes. Factor growth alone is not spending safety. |
| Long/short and private fund | Products economics/conditional fund; Household and Markets profiles | Explicit dealing, margin/recourse, liquidity and independently supplied tax facts. Legal eligibility and automatic K-1 reconstruction are not inferred. |
| Traditional exchange fund | Products / Traditional exchange funds | Conditional acceptance/lockup/fees and independent bases; eventual taxable basket sale. Tax deferral is not elimination. |
| Options | Products overlays; actual-option Household and bounded Markets paths | Leg marks, assignment, collateral and supplied tax classification; no historical option-surface inference. |
| Whole life / saving | Products illustration, actual contract and matched saving comparisons; Household loaders | Supplied schedule/contract/MEC assumptions, actual loans/taxes and separate protection cost. DIR is not policy IRR; marked wealth differs from completed exit wealth. |
| CLI and local reports | `python -m wealthlab project`, `compare`, `optimize`, `serve`; complete household/country projection and finite-search export/import | Reexecutes complete original inputs through the same household, native country or finite-grid engine as the UI. No bilateral tax certificate or global-optimum claim. |
| Opportunity-zone funds | Products/QOZ household, shared asset/source-sale paths and Optimize subscription profiles | Conditional direct-source-gain QOF C-corporation profile, actual source-year revisions, dates, fees and settlement. No inferred pass-through terms. Complete source-backed QOZ subscription and liquid-reference menus also participate conditionally in Optimize; source eligibility and C-corporation terms remain explicit. |
| Drilling | Products/Drilling, shared commodity/reference paths and Optimize participation profiles | Complete opaque source-supplied cash/tax profiles; unchanged original capital, native creditors/exits and confirmed input envelope. Full-tax drilling optimization is unsupported; supplied source envelopes remain binding. |
| Puerto Rico | Cross-border / Country cash; native PR source/gain/credit profiles | Residency, source and incentive eligibility remain explicit conditional inputs; no universal exemption is inferred. |
| Spain | Cross-border / Country cash; complete Spain inputs and report replay | Common-regime assessments retain the independent Spain context. Foreign relief stays explicit; no automatic bilateral credits or second cash book. |
| Cross-border | Country cash, shared payment/refund/funding/credit records and aligned UI/CLI comparisons | Complete PR/Spain inputs reexecute both country assessments and actual shared cash. Archived outcomes are ignored; relocation is not an automatic search control. |
| Advisory fees | Household/Markets fee editors and Optimize common-contract execution | Sourced coverage, rate method, billing fractions, observed snapshots and actual paid/owed cash; embedded costs remain separate. No provider terms are inferred. |
| Bounded optimizer | Optimize; CLI `optimize`; finite locked grids, age100 cash/stock starter and input/audit replay | Spending safety before after-tax wealth, distinct training/validation paths and actual exits. Supports integrated account/sleeve/options/exchange/life/drilling/QOZ controls; actual common advisory fees execute on each native path. Fixed PR/Spain contexts recalculate country tax for each path, with explicit monthly USD W2 and fiscal USD bank-cash links; see [fixed-country scope](adr/0220-fixed-country-context-in-policy-search.md). Automatic relocation and unmapped foreign income/basis/fiscal-wealth changes remain unsupported. Fixed native property context uses its actual closing audit; retained equity is not completed-exit cash. |
| Complete release | [Issue #19](https://github.com/pavelkrotkov/wealthlab/issues/19) | The local guide, private-input checklist and executed workflow report accompany the supported models. Acceptance requires current-head CI and independent review; synthetic success does not establish personal eligibility. |

Before relying on a result, resolve its blocked assumptions and inspect its actual enabled plans, funding failures, outstanding claims/debt, completed-exit convention and source provenance. A tax deduction, assessed credit, unexecuted exit or hypothetical preferred return never becomes spendable cash by itself.
