# Private input checklist

Keep completed answers and source records only in your explicitly selected private folder outside all public Wealthlab checkouts. This public checklist contains no personal facts. Do not send adviser communications, account records or reports to review bots. Credentials are unnecessary and must not be entered.

- Confirm each person's birth/retirement/claim dates, ownership, filing/residency profile and any dated changes. A place name or citizenship label does not establish tax eligibility.
- Reconcile opening balances with the chosen start date. Enter YTD income/taxes once, consistently with cash already reflected in those balances. Resolve conflicting spending/balance records before projection.
- Separate essential, discretionary, healthcare, mortgage and education spending; identify which debts/bills already appear elsewhere so they are not paid twice. Confirm inflation, liquidity reserve and horizon assumptions.
- For each account, confirm owner, wrapper, currency, liquidity restrictions, actual positions, acquisition dates and separate federal/state/foreign basis. Identify cross-account/spouse/wrapper restrictions before harvesting or sales.
- Supply contribution eligibility, withdrawal rights, basis/history, RMD group completeness, benefit records and withholding/payment observations for the supported retirement profile. Unknown history must not become a zero basis or automatic exception.
- Distinguish actual cash distributions from vesting, marks, taxable allocations and withholding. Confirm the source and dates for each independently supplied tax character and limitation.
- Replace synthetic product terms only from the owning contract/illustration: capital calls, fees, recourse, dealing/settlement, lockup, claims, surrender/loan/MEC terms and complete-exit assumptions. Leave missing values unresolved.
- For international cases, retain source-income identity, jurisdiction classification, acquisition/disposal FX, fiscal valuation dates, liability year, actual payment/refund dates and original recognized-tax reversals. US basis and foreign basis are separate facts.
- Confirm whether each future rule/path is a supplied assumption, frozen policy, historical input with redistribution rights or stochastic sample. Record seeds, comparison capital/flows, constraints and source versions; do not infer probabilities or manager performance from a synthetic example.
- Save a new private scenario name, keep the baseline, export the audit, and reproduce with the same clean source/dependency versions. Resolve warnings and unsupported combinations before interpreting an after-tax or retirement-safety conclusion.

See the [coverage matrix](model-coverage.md), [local workflow](local-user-guide.md) and owning source/ADR links. Completion of this checklist does not establish legal eligibility or fill missing facts automatically.
